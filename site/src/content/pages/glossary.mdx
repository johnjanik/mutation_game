This page lists the words this site uses and, beside each, the words other authors use for the same thing. Each entry starts with the site's own word and a short definition. Three lines follow it. The first gives N. J. Wildberger's word in his texts on the mutation game (<Cite k="W20"/>, <Cite k="W-MS"/>). The second gives the word of the literature on the numbers game. The third gives the standard name in Lie theory. A line left empty means that the source has no word for the thing. The last line links to the module, and the beat of it, where the word is first used.
