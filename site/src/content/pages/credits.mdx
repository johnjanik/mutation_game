## The games and the story

The mutation game is N. J. Wildberger's (<Cite k="W20"/>, <Cite k="W-MS"/>). So are the words city and population for its board and positions, and the story told with them: Martians and anti-Martians living in the cities of Mars, and Venus for the dual game. This site retells that story with his credit, from <Cite k="W20"/> section 2 and <Cite k="W-MS"/> pp. 1 and 30, and uses his terminology throughout.

The numbers game is Mozes' (<Cite k="Moz90"/>), with Proctor's earlier use of the same process (<Cite k="Pro84"/>) and Eriksson's development of it (<Cite k="Eri92"/>) acknowledged.

Ideas, rules, theorems and data from the literature are restated on this site in its own words, with citations by key to the sources page. No sentence, figure or table layout is copied from a paper, preprint or lecture. Videos are linked, or embedded with the platform's own player, and never hosted here.
