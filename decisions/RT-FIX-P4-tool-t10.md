## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-tool-t10: the squared edge lengths are exact integers (P4-RT-04-04)
Decision: the squared lengths T10 shows (`data-out="edgeLengths"`, its `data-count` and the line's per-node list) and exports (`edgeLengthsSquared`) are a_i^2 (alpha_i, alpha_i) = 2 d_i a_i^2, computed in the board's arithmetic (`SafeInt` on the named types, every result checked) from the diagonal of `gram` (`t10/uniform.ts` `exactEdgeLengths`, `shownEdgeLengths`); under make uniform, a_i = c / sqrt(d_i), so every one is 2 c^2, computed the same way. `baseEdgeLengths` keeps its signature: exact for a start of whole numbers, measured on y = P a for a real start (the unit tests still measure the uniform picture). `lengthText` writes an integer as its digits. A length of more than 12 digits (rings from about 707107) is shown by T1's `shortValue`, with the exact digits in the `title` of `data-out="edgeLengths"` (the tooltip) and in the export (SPEC 4.0 Long numbers). This replaces, in the entry "2026-10-17 P4-A3: make uniform", the words "The lengths shown are measured on the drawn coordinates y = E C^-1 a and agree with 2 d_i a_i^2 within 1e-9". What the specification left open: nothing (2.14 [V]; 4.0 Long numbers [D]). Adoption: the lens's browser case for r=999999.999999.999999 asserted the 13-digit text `1999996000002`, which 4.0 Long numbers (cited in the finding's own expected) does not show; the adopted test asserts `data-count` 1 as the lens did, the shortened text, the exact digits in the tooltip and the number 1999996000002 in the export. The lens's unit test (three cases) and the 1306 browser case are adopted unchanged.

## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-tool-t10: a j angle of any length (P4-RT-04-01)
Decision: `parseProjection` (`site/src/components/draw/projector.ts`) keeps an angle as parsed when it is a safe integer, and otherwise keeps its remainder modulo 360, computed exactly with BigInt (sign kept), which turns the picture the same way; so `j=xyz:1,2,3@9007199254740993,0,0` shows and writes back 33 and an angle of 400 nines 279. The codec still accepts any number of digits (SPEC 6.6 sets no bound on j). This amends the entry "2026-10-17 P4-A2: the j value" ("Angles are kept as parsed"): kept as parsed while a number holds them exactly. What the specification left open: whether a long angle is read or refused; the finding and both verifiers accepted either. Default used: none of section 10. Reason: 6.6 calls the angles "whole numbers of degrees" without a bound, and the codec is another cluster's file.
