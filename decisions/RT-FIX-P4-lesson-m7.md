## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-lesson-m7: the provenance of the edge-length rule (P4-RT-08-08)
Decision: beat 5 reads "The edge from lambda to the position after firing node i has squared length a_i^2(alpha_i,alpha_i)=2d_ia_i^2, with a_i the ring and d_i the weight of node i, verified by computation on A3, B3, C3, D4, F4, G2 and E6. So a polytope is uniform, with all its edges of one length, only when a_i sqrt(d_i) is the same on every ringed node." The range is the one `reference/verify.py` section 11 checks (eight random dominant starts with entries 0 to 3 on each of those seven types). The finding proposed "every board of finite type of rank at most eight", which would state a wider range than the check, the cause of P4-RT-08-01; it is not followed. The uniform condition is given as a consequence of the rule ("So"), since the base edges are edges of the polytope. What the specification left open: the wording; SPEC 2.14 labels the rule [V] with no range, and 0.3 asks for the range checked. Reason: finding P4-RT-08-08.
