## 2026-10-25 RT-FIX-P5-t12-content: the text of the panel of the move beside its signed version (P5-RT-09-01)
Decision: the panel's sentences depend on m, the largest number at node k in the orbit (`largestAt`; written by `signedNote`). When m is at most 1, as on every minuscule node: `Firing node k permutes the positions: its matrix is E_k + F_k + P_k, where P_k keeps the positions whose number at node k is 0. The group element exp(E_k) exp(−F_k) exp(E_k) is E_k − F_k + P_k: the same matrix with one sign changed in each pair. Its square multiplies each position by −1 to the power of its number at node k.` Otherwise: `Some position has the number m at node k, and firing node k moves it m steps at once, which the single steps E_k and F_k miss. So the matrix of the move is not E_k + F_k + P_k, where P_k keeps the positions whose number at node k is 0, and the signed matrix E_k − F_k + P_k is not exp(E_k) exp(−F_k) exp(E_k).` The condition is exact: firing moves mu by mu_k steps, so the firing matrix is E_k + F_k + P_k^0 exactly when every |mu_k| is at most 1; then the orbit splits under node k into pairs (mu_k = 1 and −1) and fixed positions (mu_k = 0), on which (I + E_k)(I − F_k)(I + E_k) = E_k − F_k + P_k^0 and its square is (−1)^{H_k}; a position with |mu_k| of 2 or more lies outside every entry of E_k, F_k and P_k^0, so E_k − F_k + P_k^0 is singular while the exponentials are invertible. The square sentence is therefore also shown on the nodes that are not minuscule where m is at most 1 (B3 node 1 at k = 1, for example), where it holds; the regression test checks every claim against the engine's matrices at every k of every built node of A3, B2, B3, C3, D4, D5, G2 and F4. The sentences are markup built from numbers and fixed words only, shown with {@html} (the same text written with fourteen subscripts in the template took /tools/rep/ over its 90 kB). The previous text (decisions/P5-A2.md records only the panel's order and content, "panels, presets, link keys") is replaced. What the specification left open: SPEC 4 T12 names the panel ("the move matrix beside its signed version") and not its text. Reason: finding P5-RT-09-01.

## 2026-10-25 RT-FIX-P5-t12-content: "Dimension" and the positions of the orbit (P5-RT-09-02, P5-RT-13-03)
Decision: the facts line shows `Dimension: N` with N the dimension of the representation whose highest weight is the fundamental position of the node (`representationDimension`, the engine's `weylDimension`; `data-out="dimension"`; 28 on D4 node 2, 248 on E8 node 8, 3875 on E8 node 1), and beside it `Positions in the orbit: M` with M the orbit's size, which is also the size of the matrices T12 builds (`data-out="orbit-size"`, a new name in `T12_OUTS` and in the comment block of `T12.spec.ts`). On a minuscule node the two are equal (F11); elsewhere the dimension is larger, by the positions the orbit leaves out. Download data's `dimension` is the representation's dimension on every node (it was the basis size, null on a node not built); `orbitSize` is unchanged. The SPEC uses "dimension" in two senses, the size of the matrices (4 T12 "as lists of nonzero entries above dimension 27"; 5.6 "Dimension for matrix grids | 27") and dim V(lambda) (4 T12 acceptance; 2.7 F9); which one a reader-facing "Dimension" should be is for the owner. Options: (a) as done, both numbers each under the name of what it counts; (b) "Dimension" on a minuscule node only and the positions of the orbit alone elsewhere (the finding's proposedFix; the reproducers of both findings then fail, since they read `data-out="dimension"` on nodes that are not minuscule); (c) (a) with a sentence on the positions the orbit leaves out. Recommendation: (a), which keeps "Dimension" for the representation's dimension, as the coordinator asked, and lets the reader set T12 against M8's 248 and 26. `weylDimension` was already in the engine chunk that /tools/rep/ loads (shared with T5), so the import costs a few bytes. This adds to decisions/P5-A2.md, "data-out names and the selects" (the name `orbit-size`) and changes the meaning of `dimension` in "Download data". What the specification left open: the readouts of T12. Reason: findings P5-RT-09-02 and P5-RT-13-03.
