## 2026-10-25 P6-A3: the default root
Decision: the first positive root, in the order of `positiveRoots` (total, then lexicographic), that is not a root of the black sub-board (its support reaches a white node); on the compact form, the first positive root. It gives (0, 1, 0) for su*(4), (0, 0, 1) for su(1,3) and alpha_6 for e6(-26). The root picker lists the positive roots and then their negatives; choosing one resets the play. ## 2026-10-25 P6-A3: the Reason sentences and the failure note
Decision: one sentence per red light, written from the engine's Reason record with labels (positions converted with toLabels), subscripts and superscripts as markup: `not-a-symmetry` "τ is not a symmetry of the board (it breaks the roads at nodes 1 and 2)."; `not-an-involution` "τ applied twice is not the identity (nodes 1, 2 and 3)."; `moves-black-set` "τ sends black node 1 to a white node, so it does not keep the black set." (plural: "black nodes 1 and 2 to white nodes"); `differs-from-minus-wX` "On the black node 1, τ differs from −w_X." (plural "black nodes"); `not-an-integer` "At node 2 the number ⟨α_2, ρ_X^∨⟩ is −1/2, which is not an integer.", and with several nodes one clause per node in the order of the nodes, reading the value as the fractions joined by "; " (MERGE ruling (2) on P6-A1): "At node 3 the number ⟨α_3, ρ_X^∨⟩ is −3/2 and at node 5 the number ⟨α_5, ρ_X^∨⟩ is −1/2, which are not integers." Each light states its condition in one line and its verdict as `holds` or `fails`, with a disc or a cross beside it (A-5). The failure note: always "This decoration is not the Satake diagram of any real form, so the Studio shows no data for it."; when conditions 1 and 2 hold and condition 3 fails, also "Conditions 1 and 2 hold, and θ = −w_X∘τ can still be played: it permutes the roots, and playing it twice gives back the root. But no involution of the Lie algebra acts as this θ on the Cartan subalgebra and fixes the root vectors of the black nodes." The unit test checks on every such pair of rank at most 4 that theta permutes the roots and is an involution, so the sentence holds; no text of the tool says that theta is not an involution, and the [U] sentence on sl(3,R) and Vogan diagrams (E15) is never printed. The two optional facts on A2 with node 1 black (order 4, dim k 5) are not printed (they are not computed by the engine). Default used: the plan's wording (instruction 5.4, 5.5), the second sentence of the note reworded so that it says what is true for every such pair. Reason: SPEC 2.15 "What a failed condition 3 means"; M9 beat 4.

## 2026-10-25 P6-A3: the C2 drawing rule
Decision: `assembleData` builds one complete record of SPEC 6.4 per record `matchCatalogue` returns, with the numbers of `satake` on the reader's own pair and the names, Cartan's label and `restricted_type_display` of the record; the C2 adjustment is applied record by record (where the record displays C2 and satake says B2, the restricted board is named C2 and satake's orbits in Bourbaki order are reversed). The restricted board is drawn by the record whose black nodes and tau equal the reader's pair, otherwise by the first record: on D4 with black nodes 3 and 4 that is so(2,6), drawn B2; with black nodes 1 and 3 it is so*(8), drawn C2; with black nodes 1 and 4 (a pair of the class that is no record's own) so(2,6). The drawn board is `boardFromName` of that name, and its node with label k stands for the k-th orbit of the record's Bourbaki list; values, pairs, composite words and reduced lengths are found by orbit in `rb.orbits`, never by index (on e6(-14) drawn node 1 is the orbit {2}, the second in orbit order). The unit test checks, on the 135 records of positive real rank, that the drawn board's Cartan matrix read through the orbits equals the golden `restricted_cartan`. The plan's signature `assembleData(sat, rb, records, board)` takes the diagram instead of the board, since the record fields black_nodes, tau and arrows are the reader's pair. What the specification left open: which record decides the drawing when two match. Default used: the plan's (instruction 5.6). Reason: SPEC 6.4 "Names in rank 2"; an open question for MERGE.

## 2026-10-25 P6-A3: the values on the restricted board's nodes and the shadow coordinates
Decision: the coordinates of the chosen population's shadow in the simple restricted roots come from the engine's `restrictedCoordinates(d, rb, shadow2(d, p))` (P6-A1's follow-up, merged at cac8b1e; one integer per orbit in the order of `rb.orbits`), read by orbit into the Bourbaki order of the drawn board. ## 2026-10-25 P6-A3: where the Satake data are computed
Decision: `satake`, `restrictedBoard` and `matchCatalogue` go through `engineCall` with `{ board, kind: 'satake' }` (t13/load.ts, which provides the three by name), so that `whereToRun` sends E7 and E8 to the worker and keeps every other board on the main thread; `satakeRecord` is not called (the records come from `matchCatalogue`). On the main thread the three calls run each in a task of its own after the task that draws the decoration and the lights; in the worker they are posted together. A pair with no white node gets the empty restricted board without a call. `admissibility`, `theta`, `shadow2`, `restrictedCoordinates`, `satakeCatalogue`, `canonicalPair`, `positiveRoots`, `symmetries` and `mutate` run on the main thread. The server render computes the data at once (Node, where E-9 does not apply), and so does the first render in the browser on a main-thread board, so that hydration finds the server's markup; on E7 and E8 the browser always asks the worker (an instance configured on E7 or E8 shows "computing the data in the background" for a moment after hydration). While the data are computed, the lights are shown and the data panel says "computing the data in the background". Measured in Chromium with the processor slowed four times (scratch scripts, not committed; a shared, loaded machine): picking D8 gave long tasks of 255, 96 and 53 ms (the first is the picker's change with its rendering, the others satake and matchCatalogue; before the split one run gave 214 and 246 ms), picking E6 76 and 50 ms, painting a node of D8 none, opening the shadows of e6(-26) 75 ms; no long task after loading the default route. In Node, warm, satake takes 11.8 ms and matchCatalogue 8.3 ms on the split form of D8. Open question for MERGE: whether E-9 should also send Satake data of the larger boards (rank 7 and 8 of A to D) to the worker. Default used: E-9 as written, the plan's routing of matchCatalogue (instruction 5.12). Reason: SPEC 5.1 E-9, 7.6; open question for MERGE below.

## 2026-10-25 P6-A3: the panels and the history
Decision: the decoration, the game board with the root picker and History, and the three lights are always shown; one panel below them is open at a time, as WAI-ARIA tabs with automatic activation: Examples (the `lights` preset), Data, Play θ, Shadows, Restricted board, Catalogue. The table view holds the decoration with one paint button per node, the lights, the restricted board with one button per node and the multiplicities by length class. A Download data file holds the verdicts with node labels, the records of each matched name in labels and the shadow rows. The per-row LaTeX copy of the catalogue (a plan option) is not built: loading a row and pressing Export LaTeX gives its `\dynkin` line. Default used: the plan's where SPEC is silent. Reason: SPEC 4.0 History, A-9, A-10.
