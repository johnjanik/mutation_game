## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the depth axis of the Coxeter view and the three turns
Decision: the Coxeter view is coordinates 0 (right) and n-1 (up) of y with coordinate 1 as depth for n >= 3 (toward the viewer is positive), and none for n = 2. The turning angles of the j value act on the frame in the order horizontal axis u (turn[0]), vertical axis v (turn[1]), viewing axis w (turn[2]), each about the axes as they stand after the previous turn. Signs follow the right-hand rule for turning the picture, with u, v, w right-handed: a positive turn[0] brings the top toward the viewer, a positive turn[1] takes what faced the viewer to the right, a positive turn[2] turns the picture counterclockwise on the screen. Each step turns two orthonormal vectors in their own plane (angles reduced modulo 360 before conversion to radians; a multiple of 360 is skipped, so the frame stays bit-exact), which keeps the frame orthonormal to 1e-12 (tested for n = 2 to 8). What the specification left open: SPEC 6.6 names the three axes but not the order, the signs, or which coordinate is depth in the Coxeter view. Default used: the plan's (instruction 5.3). Reason: the round trip "a turn, then its negation in reverse order" is the identity, and the picture of the oracle's `coxeter_plane_points` is the view at zero turn.

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: rank 2 and rank 1
Decision: on rank 2 `frameFor` returns `w: null` (never a zero vector) and `turnFrame` applies only turn[2]; turn[0] and turn[1] leave the frame unchanged, so the picture stays flat and u, v stay orthonormal. `parseProjection` still accepts three angles on rank 2 and `formatProjection` writes back what was parsed. Plan extension: rank 1 (A1, among the named types T10 offers) is a line: `frameFor(1, coxeter)` is u = (1), v = (0), w = null, no turn moves it, `parseProjection` accepts `coxeter` (with or without angles, as `urlCodec.ts` does) and never `xyz`, and `defaultProjection(1)` is `coxeter`. What the specification left open: SPEC 4 T10 says "rank 2 flat" and nothing about rank 1. Reason: a link `g=A1&j=coxeter` passes the codec, so the projector must read it.

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: "projection preserves the invariant form on the chosen plane"
Decision: read as: in y-space the form is the dot product (y . y' = (p, p'), tested within 1e-9 against `formValue` on 50 random population pairs for A3, B3, G2 and E8 with E from `choleskyBasis`, and from `euclideanBasis` too once the engine exports it); rotations in coordinate planes and the turns are isometries (dot products unchanged within 1e-12); the frame is orthonormal, so a vector y = s u + t v of the chosen plane is drawn at (s, t) with length preserved, and on rank 3 with xyz:1,2,3 the three coordinates hold the whole length. What the specification left open: the roster's wording. Default used: the plan's interpretation (5.3).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the depth cue, its alpha floor, eight levels
Decision: `depthStyle(depth)` for depth 0 (far) to 1 (near) is width = lineWidth x (0.5 + d), radius = pointRadius x (0.6 + 0.8 d), alpha = 0.6 + 0.4 d, linear and so continuous and non-decreasing; `depthStyle(null)` (no depth axis) is the middle (d = 1/2). Base values: line width 1 CSS pixel, point radius 2.5 (plan). The alpha floor is 0.6: the road and text colours of both themes blended at 0.6 over their page keep 4.5 to 1 or more against it (light road 4.54, dark road 6.02 by the WCAG formula with sRGB blending; tested at 3 to 1, A-6), so a far segment is still a road. The cue works through width and radius first. Renderers quantise into eight levels (`DEPTH_LEVELS`), each drawn with `depthStyle` at the centre of the level, rounded to two decimals so that canvas and SVG carry identical numbers; a scene without depth uses the middle style for every level. What the specification left open: the mapping (T10 "points and segments with a depth cue"). Default used: the plan's (5.4, 5.6).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the scale of buildScene and the depth of a scene
Decision: `buildScene` puts the origin of y at the centre of the box, one scale for both directions, y flipped once (downwards on the screen); the scale is (min(width, height) / 2 - margin) / radius with radius by default the largest norm of the unprojected vectors, which `project` records in `Point3.norm` (plan extension of `Point3`), so that no rotation or turn changes it and a picture does not jump while it is turned; margin defaults to the radius of the nearest point plus 2 pixels. Depth is z normalised over the scene's z range; a scene has no depth axis when the cue is off or when the z range is below 1e-12 times the radius (rank 2: every z is 0), and then every depth is 1/2. Segments carry the mean depth of their ends and are sorted far to near, ties in the order given (stable). `Scene` also carries `scale` (pixels per unit of y) for T10's pointer mapping; the renderers do not read it. What the specification left open: everything here. Default used: the plan's (5.5).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the caps while turning, and above 70000 edges
Decision: `selectSegments(scene, turning)`: above `CAPS.edgesAtRest` segments nothing is drawn but the vertices, at rest and while turning alike (beyond that cap the edges are not enumerated at all, T10 "Limits"); at rest everything up to that cap; while turning everything up to `CAPS.edgesTurning`, otherwise exactly `CAPS.edgesTurning` chosen by the fixed stride floor(k m / cap), k = 0 .. cap - 1, through the far to near order, the same every frame. `drawScene` draws the first `CAPS.verticesDrawn` points in scene order. The caps are read from the engine's `CAPS` (exported again as `LIMITS` for the tests); a unit test scans the three modules for a retyped cap. What the specification left open: how the turning sample is chosen. Default used: the plan's (5.6).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: colours, background and the canvas context
Decision: segments use `--road`, points `--text`, the background `--page` (plan; CONTRACTS 8 lists the tokens but assigns none to T10's marks and SPEC 6.3 has no such mapping). `drawScene` receives the three concrete strings (T10 reads them with `getComputedStyle` at draw time and on a theme change); `renderSvg` writes `var(--road)`, `var(--text)` and `var(--page)` by default, or concrete strings for a file or an export. Neither module contains a colour literal (tested). The canvas fills its background with the page colour (`fillRect`, added to the plan's list of `Context2DLike` members) so that the picture is the same as the SVG's background rectangle; `strokeStyle` and `fillStyle` are typed `unknown` in `Context2DLike` so that a real `CanvasRenderingContext2D` fits. The device pixel ratio is applied once with `setTransform(dpr, 0, 0, dpr, 0, 0)` inside one `save`/`restore`. What the specification left open: the tokens of T10's marks. Default used: the plan's (5.6, 5.7).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the j value
Decision: `parseProjection(text, n)` uses the same regular expression as `site/src/lib/urlCodec.ts` (`coxeter` or `xyz:` and three digit strings, optionally `@` and three integers, negative allowed) and adds the rank rules: xyz axes distinct and in 1..n (so never on rank 1 or 2); it returns null otherwise. It agrees with the codec on every input the codec accepts except that the codec does not check rank (it accepts `coxeter` everywhere, as the projector does) and both accept leading zeros (`xyz:01,2,3`). `formatProjection` writes axes from 1, angles reduced to 0..359, and leaves out `@0,0,0`; `formatProjection(parseProjection(s, n)) === s` for every normalised s. Angles are kept as parsed (for example -30) and reduced only when written or turned. What the specification left open: normalisation. Default used: the plan's (5.3).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the SVG snapshot and its 200 kB guard
Decision: `renderSvg(scene, options)` returns one `<svg xmlns=... viewBox="0 0 W H" width height>` string: optional `<title>` and `<desc>` first (then `role="img"` and `aria-labelledby`), a background `<rect>`, one `<g>` of segments with one `<path stroke-width stroke-opacity d="Mx yLx y...">` per depth level far first, and one `<g>` of points with one `<g fill-opacity>` per level holding one `<circle cx cy r>` per point; every id is prefixed by `options.id` (default `projection`); coordinates rounded with `roundTo(x, decimals)` (default one decimal). It uses the same `selectSegments(scene, false)`, `levelStyles`, `segmentRuns` and `pointPlan` as the canvas, and a test checks that the canvas and the SVG receive the same rounded coordinates, widths, radii and opacities in the same order. Measured: the E8 root polytope (240 points, 6720 segments, depth cue off) in a 360 by 360 box is 135761 bytes at one decimal, under the 200 kB guard. Because P4-A1's `euclideanBasis` and the committed E8 file do not exist yet, that test builds the scene from the engine (`roots`, and the pairs at inner product 1 through the integral Gram matrix, a sample checked with `formValue`, every degree equal to the golden 56) with `choleskyBasis` in place of `euclideanBasis`; it switches to `euclideanBasis` by itself once the engine exports it, and the continuation may switch its source to the committed E8 file as the plan says. The size in the true Coxeter plane will differ by a few kilobytes and is to be re-measured then. What the specification left open: the markup (7.4). Default used: the plan's (5.7).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: distinct dots and rings
Decision: `roundTo(x, digits) = Math.round(x * 10^digits) / 10^digits + 0` (never -0); `countDistinct` keys on both coordinates rounded to 6 decimals; `ringsOf` keys on the radius `Math.hypot(x, y)` of the unrounded coordinates rounded to 6 decimals, sorted innermost first: the two different rules of the oracle's `coxeter_plane_rings`. This is not bit for bit Python's `round` (half to even on the exact binary value); the two can differ only within about 1e-12 of a rounding boundary. What the specification left open: the JavaScript rounding. Default used: the plan's (5.8).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the preset file format, key naming and the configurations
Decision: `PRESET_CONFIGS` holds the seven configurations of the M7 figure table in this order: A2 highest root (`root-polytope`, `two-graphs`), A3 highest root (`root-polytope`), D4 highest root (`root-polytope`, `coxeter-plane`), A3 (1,0,0) (`rings`), B3 (1,0,1) (`face-table`), B3 (0,1,1) (`make-uniform`), E8 highest root (`showpiece`); the rings of a root polytope are computed as `toNumbers(board, highestRoot(board))`, never typed. Keys `<board>-r<rings joined by '.'>` (A2-r1.1, A3-r1.0.1, D4-r0.1.0.0, A3-r1.0.0, B3-r1.0.1, B3-r0.1.1, E8-r0.0.0.0.0.0.0.1, checked by loading the module in Node). A file holds, in this order and nothing else: schema, tool, key, board, rings, presets, counts {vertices, edges, moveEdges}, edgesEnumerated, fVector (numbers when safe, else decimal strings), vertices, adjacency and moveAdjacency (for each vertex the sorted larger neighbours), tree {parent, node as labels}, shortRoots (null on all seven, which are simply laced). `serializePreset` is `JSON.stringify` with no spaces and a final newline; the manifest `index.json` is `{schema, tool, files: [{key, board, rings, presets, file, counts}]}` with bare file names. A repeated pair, a loop, or an edge end that is not a vertex throws; a truncated orbit or edge enumeration throws `PresetRefused`. The default configuration of T10 is P4-A3's to fix (deviation, see the report). The measured sizes of adjacency lists against pairs, and the committed files themselves, wait for P4-A1 and the continuation. What the specification left open: the format (plan). Default used: the plan's (5.9).
