## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the depth axis of the Coxeter view and the three turns
Decision: the Coxeter view is coordinates 0 (right) and n-1 (up) of y with coordinate 1 as depth for n >= 3 (toward the viewer is positive), and none for n = 2. The turning angles of the j value act on the frame in the order horizontal axis u (turn[0]), vertical axis v (turn[1]), viewing axis w (turn[2]), each about the axes as they stand after the previous turn. Signs follow the right-hand rule for turning the picture, with u, v, w right-handed: a positive turn[0] brings the top toward the viewer, a positive turn[1] takes what faced the viewer to the right, a positive turn[2] turns the picture counterclockwise on the screen. Each step turns two orthonormal vectors in their own plane (angles reduced modulo 360 before conversion to radians; a multiple of 360 is skipped, so the frame stays bit-exact), which keeps the frame orthonormal to 1e-12 (tested for n = 2 to 8). What the specification left open: SPEC 6.6 names the three axes but not the order, the signs, or which coordinate is depth in the Coxeter view. Default used: the plan's (instruction 5.3). Reason: the round trip "a turn, then its negation in reverse order" is the identity, and the picture of the oracle's `coxeter_plane_points` is the view at zero turn.

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: rank 2 and rank 1
Decision: on rank 2 `frameFor` returns `w: null` (never a zero vector) and `turnFrame` applies only turn[2]; turn[0] and turn[1] leave the frame unchanged, so the picture stays flat and u, v stay orthonormal. `parseProjection` still accepts three angles on rank 2 and `formatProjection` writes back what was parsed. Plan extension: rank 1 (A1, among the named types T10 offers) is a line: `frameFor(1, coxeter)` is u = (1), v = (0), w = null, no turn moves it, `parseProjection` accepts `coxeter` (with or without angles, as `urlCodec.ts` does) and never `xyz`, and `defaultProjection(1)` is `coxeter`. What the specification left open: SPEC 4 T10 says "rank 2 flat" and nothing about rank 1. Reason: a link `g=A1&j=coxeter` passes the codec, so the projector must read it.

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: "projection preserves the invariant form on the chosen plane"

What the specification left open: the roster's wording. Default used: the plan's interpretation (5.3).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the depth cue, its alpha floor, eight levels
Decision: `depthStyle(depth)` for depth 0 (far) to 1 (near) is width = lineWidth x (0.5 + d), radius = pointRadius x (0.6 + 0.8 d), alpha = 0.6 + 0.4 d, linear and so continuous and non-decreasing; `depthStyle(null)` (no depth axis) is the middle (d = 1/2). Base values: line width 1 CSS pixel, point radius 2.5 (plan). The alpha floor is 0.6: the road and text colours of both themes blended at 0.6 over their page keep 4.5 to 1 or more against it (light road 4.54, dark road 6.02 by the WCAG formula with sRGB blending; tested at 3 to 1, A-6), so a far segment is still a road. The cue works through width and radius first. Renderers quantise into eight levels (`DEPTH_LEVELS`), each drawn with `depthStyle` at the centre of the level, rounded to two decimals so that canvas and SVG carry identical numbers; a scene without depth uses the middle style for every level. What the specification left open: the mapping (T10 "points and segments with a depth cue"). Default used: the plan's (5.4, 5.6).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the scale of buildScene and the depth of a scene
Decision: `buildScene` puts the origin of y at the centre of the box, one scale for both directions, y flipped once (downwards on the screen); the scale is (min(width, height) / 2 - margin) / radius with radius by default the largest norm of the unprojected vectors, which `project` records in `Point3.norm` (plan extension of `Point3`), so that no rotation or turn changes it and a picture does not jump while it is turned; margin defaults to the radius of the nearest point plus 2 pixels. Depth is z normalised over the scene's z range; a scene has no depth axis when the cue is off or when the z range is below 1e-12 times the radius (rank 2: every z is 0), and then every depth is 1/2. Segments carry the mean depth of their ends and are sorted far to near, ties in the order given (stable). `Scene` also carries `scale` (pixels per unit of y) for T10's pointer mapping; the renderers do not read it. What the specification left open: everything here. Default used: the plan's (5.5).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the caps while turning, and above 70000 edges

The caps are read from the engine's `CAPS` (exported again as `LIMITS` for the tests); a unit test scans the three modules for a retyped cap. What the specification left open: how the turning sample is chosen. Default used: the plan's (5.6).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the j value
Decision: `parseProjection(text, n)` uses the same regular expression as `site/src/lib/urlCodec.ts` (`coxeter` or `xyz:` and three digit strings, optionally `@` and three integers, negative allowed) and adds the rank rules: xyz axes distinct and in 1..n (so never on rank 1 or 2); it returns null otherwise. It agrees with the codec on every input the codec accepts except that the codec does not check rank (it accepts `coxeter` everywhere, as the projector does) and both accept leading zeros (`xyz:01,2,3`). `formatProjection` writes axes from 1, angles reduced to 0..359, and leaves out `@0,0,0`; `formatProjection(parseProjection(s, n)) === s` for every normalised s. Angles are kept as parsed (for example -30) and reduced only when written or turned. What the specification left open: normalisation. Default used: the plan's (5.3).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: the SVG snapshot and its 200 kB guard

## 2026-10-17 P4-A2: distinct dots and rings
Decision: `roundTo(x, digits) = Math.round(x * 10^digits) / 10^digits + 0` (never -0); `countDistinct` keys on both coordinates rounded to 6 decimals; `ringsOf` keys on the radius `Math.hypot(x, y)` of the unrounded coordinates rounded to 6 decimals, sorted innermost first: the two different rules of the oracle's `coxeter_plane_rings`.
