# Decisions of RT-FIX-P6-latex (the LaTeX export of the tools, phase 6 red-team round)

## 2026-10-26 RT-FIX-P6-latex: T8 and T12 export their orbit graphs as a tikz-graph (P6-RT-10-01)
Decision: the Weyl Explorer (T8) and the Representation Builder (T12) pass ToolFrame's `latexForExport` a function, read at the click, that writes the orbit graph as drawn, in the `tikz-graph` form of decisions/P6-A5.md and the way P6-tikz writes T2's garden (decisions/P6-tikz.md): coordinates in centimetres at 96 CSS pixels to the inch, y negated (y up, layer 0 on top), the whole picture scaled down to 16 cm when wider; `directed: false`, since both drawings show each move as a line with no arrowhead and the layers show the direction. T8 (new pure module `site/src/components/tools/t08/latex.ts`, `weylLatex`): every dot of the scene of `t08/layout.ts` `buildScene`, in its order (`model.drawn`, the order of the `data-out="vertex"` hooks), at its centre, with no label because the dots carry none; then the edges of the scene in its order, that is every legal move up to 192 elements and above that the moves along the game and at the focused vertex, exactly as the SVG or the canvas draws them. T12 (new pure module `site/src/components/tools/t12/latex.ts`, `orbitLatex`): every vertex of the orbit, in the drawn order (`OrbitLayers.vertices`, the order of the `data-vertex` hooks), at the centre of its box, labelled with its position in label order, every digit, ASCII hyphen-minus and commas (the form of T2's labels; the drawing's text "(1, −1) #2" is written without the parentheses and without the basis index after `#`, which names a row of the matrices and is not part of the position); then every edge in the drawn order. The geometry of T12's boxes was moved, unchanged, out of `orbitSvgLayers` into a new exported `orbitGeometry` of `t12/OrbitLayers.svelte`, which both the drawing and the export read, so that the export cannot drift from the drawing. Not written, as for T2 (decisions/P6-tikz.md, "what the TikZ form leaves out"): the colour of an edge by node, the node label each edge carries, the path marks and the focus mark of T8, and the dash of a T12 edge that is no entry of E or F. What the specification left open: which tools draw an orbit graph, and the coordinates, labels and order of the TikZ form. Reason: SPEC 6.7 "Orbit graphs and projections are exported as plain TikZ coordinates"; SPEC 4 T8 ("the graph of all positions reachable from all ones by legal play, drawn in layers by the number of moves") and T12 ("the orbit drawn in layers with edges coloured by node") are orbit graphs; the finding's expected.

## 2026-10-26 RT-FIX-P6-latex: T12 with no orbit drawn shows the notice (P6-RT-10-01)
Decision: when T12 draws no orbit graph (no matrices built, or an orbit of more than DRAW_MAX = 300 positions, given as layer counts), its `latexForExport` answers null, so Export LaTeX shows `nothing to export as LaTeX` in `data-out="exportNotice"` and downloads nothing; it does not fall back to the board's `\dynkin` line. What the specification left open: what a tool whose figure is an orbit graph writes when none is drawn. Reason: the rule P6-tikz recorded for a null answer ("it does not fall back to the board's diagram", decisions/P6-tikz.md); the layer counts are a list, not a graph. Tested in `site/tests/e2e/latex-export.spec.ts` on E7 node 2.

## 2026-10-26 RT-FIX-P6-latex: the writers of T8 and T12 are fetched on the click (P6-RT-10-01)
Decision: ToolFrame's prop `latexForExport` (decisions/P6-A5.md: `LatexChoice | (() => LatexChoice | null)`) also accepts a function that answers with a promise of the same (`() => LatexChoice | null | Promise<LatexChoice | null>`), an additive widening: the click calls the function at once, awaits its answer together with the `import()` of export.ts, and hands the answer to `latexDownload` unchanged, so a value, a function answering a value and a function answering null behave as before. T8 and T12 answer `import('./t08/latex')` and `import('./t12/latex')`, so their writers, like export.ts, are fetched only on the first click and no route fetches more before an interaction. T2's and T10's writers stay as P6-tikz made them (imported with the tool, about 200 bytes each); moving them is not needed by this round's findings. Deviation: a recorded plan prop of P6-A5 is widened (not a CONTRACTS.md interface: CONTRACTS 5.1 lists no `latexForExport`); ToolFrame.svelte is touched only in the prop's type and in `onExportLatex`. Reason: the orchestrator's note for this cluster (the writers stay behind the dynamic import of the click; SPEC 7.6 budget). The components hand the writers their own model (T8: the scene and the model's edges; T12: the orbit, the geometry and the label order), so that the loops run in the fetched module and the code each route fetches first is only the closure. Measured with `npm run size -- --port 7301` (gzip bytes, before at 8de73de, after on this branch; the build of each): `/tools/rep/`, `/embed/rep/` and `/modules/m8-lie-algebras/` 85706 to 85875 (+169: the closure, the prop, and `orbitGeometry` now returning its functions); `/tools/weyl/` and `/embed/weyl/` 77359 to 77443 (+84); every other route that hydrates a ToolFrame +17 or +18 (ToolFrame's await of the answer); the writers (`t08/latex.ts` 330 bytes and `t12/latex.ts` 404 bytes before compression) are fetched by no route before the click (`latex-export.spec.ts` > "the writers of T8 and T12, like export.ts, are fetched on the click of Export LaTeX and not before (7.6)"); 90 routes, 0 over budget before and after.

## 2026-10-26 RT-FIX-P6-latex: T6, T15 and T16 keep their export (P6-RT-10-01)
Decision: T6 (Kaleidoscope) keeps the default export of P6-A5, the `\dynkin` line of its board; T15 (Affine Playground) and T16 (Sandbox) keep `nothing to export as LaTeX`. T6: its figure (SPEC 4 T6) is "the plane with the two mirrors, the simple roots drawn as the mirror normals, the chambers, and one point", with the parallelogram of the population and the two perpendiculars of the numbers; it is neither an orbit graph nor the projection of a point set (the orbit of the point is an optional overlay, off by default), and the two TikZ forms of P6-A5 cannot write it: `tikz-points` draws a dot at every point and segments only between such dots, so a mirror, an arrow, a chamber, a perpendicular or the parallelogram would come out with dots the figure does not have, and the chambers not at all. Writing only T6's points would export a picture different from the one drawn. A faithful TikZ export of T6 needs a form for lines and polygons without dots, that is a new kind or option of `exportLaTeX` (CONTRACTS 4), which is the owner's to add; until then the board's diagram is what the `dynkin-diagrams` half of SPEC 4.0's LaTeX export gives. T15 and T16: each figure is a board (the affine and twisted boards and the two cycles of T15; the editor's board of T16) with its numbers or populations, not an orbit graph or a projection, so 6.7's TikZ sentence does not reach them; their boards are not among the 32 finite types, which P6-A5 recorded as the only ones the package is written for ("Affine, twisted, preset and custom boards have no form in SPEC 6.7 and are refused"), so the notice stays. What the specification left open: which of these figures 6.7's last paragraph governs. Reason: SPEC 4 (each tool's figure), 6.7, decisions/P6-A5.md; the orchestrator's instruction to decide and record. Option for the owner: (a) keep this; (b) export T6's points only (root tips, the point, the orbit when shown) as `tikz-points`; (c) add a TikZ form for lines and polygons and export T6's plane in full. Recommendation (a) until (c) is wanted.

## 2026-10-26 RT-FIX-P6-latex: a hole in a list the TikZ writers read (P6-RT-03-03)
Decision: `exportLaTeX` reads every list by index, 0 to length - 1 (`Array.from`), so a hole of a sparse list is read as undefined and meets the check an explicit undefined meets: a vertex, a point, an edge or an end of an edge that is a hole is refused with RangeError, naming the fault; a hole in the `labels` list of `tikz-points` writes no label, as undefined, null and the empty text do. The `dynkin` lists (`black`, `tau`) were already read with `for ... of`, which reads a hole as undefined, and so already refused one. What the specification left open: what the writers do with a hole (CONTRACTS 4 gives the signature; the plan says RangeError on data the writer cannot write). Reason: the finding's expected; decisions/P6-A5.md "an edge that does not join two given vertices" throws.
