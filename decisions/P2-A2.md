## 2026-10-10 P2-A2: the linking mechanism
Decision: no new mechanism. With `linkTo` the tool calls P1-A2's `followToolState(linkTo, ...)` (state.ts), the store P1-A6's T2 follows; the meter reads `positionsOf` of the last published state (its last position), and the tool draws no board and no History of its own (`meterModel` in `t03/model.ts` returns `ownBoard: false`). ## 2026-10-10 P2-A2: weights: integers, ranges, defaults
Decision: weights are integers; a city weight runs from 1 to 24, a road weight from 0 to 24 (`CITY_MIN`, `ROAD_MIN`, `WEIGHT_MAX` in `t03/weights.ts`). Each weight is a slider (`input type=range`, arrow keys) paired with a number field; a typed value outside the range or not a whole number is refused by `readWeight` and the field returns to its last accepted weight on blur or change. Default: every weight 1 (on a simply laced board this passes; on B2 it fails), unless the page passes `cityWeights` (label order) and `roadWeights` (by label pairs). Weights, rows and the verdict go back to their defaults when the board changes. What the specification left open: the control and its range (instruction 5.4, plan). Reason: SPEC 4 T3 "City weights are positive; road weights are zero or positive"; 24 admits every passing weight of the named types (at most 6, on G2) times 3; a zero city weight would let the one-way road "pass" (verify.py 1530).

## 2026-10-10 P2-A2: the matrix convention
Decision: `matrixOf(board, city, road)` returns the doubled integer matrix 2M, diagonal `2 w_i`, off-diagonal `-w_ij` on joined pairs (0 elsewhere), symmetric by construction, and that is exactly the matrix handed to `formIsInvariant`. With the passing weights `w_i = 2 d_i`, `w_ij = 2 d_i A_ji` it equals `2 gram(board)`, checked entry by entry in `T03.test.ts > builder accepts the symmetrizer weights on A2, B2, B3, F4, G2`. The data export says so in `builder.convention`. Reason: instruction 5.4; verify.py 1527 builds M with `Fr(-wij, 2)`; scaling does not change invariance.

## 2026-10-10 P2-A2: the digit rule: sign and paste
Decision: a field holds an optional leading minus sign (ASCII or U+2212) and at most `CAPS.formFinderDigits` digits; the sign is not counted. Every input event offers the whole new text to `offerEntry`: a text with a seventh digit, a letter or a sign elsewhere is refused whole and the field is put back to its last accepted text, so a pasted seven-digit string is refused, not truncated. Reason: SPEC 5.6 "the field refuses more"; instruction 5.5 (plan reading).

## 2026-10-10 P2-A2: a link with c=n, v or m
Decision: a link whose coords are numbers, or whose view or mode is not populations and free, is unreadable for T3: the tool loads its defaults and shows P1-A2's `LINK_NOTICE`. The state written back is exactly `{ schema, tool: 'form', board, coords: 'populations', start, moves }`. The weights, the rows and the part shown are never in a link (6.6). Reason: instruction 5.2.
