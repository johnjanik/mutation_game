## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: refusal of a start other than all ones
Decision: after `decodeState`, `admitLink` (t08/link.ts) refuses a link whose coords are not the numbers, whose start is not all ones, whose view or mode is another, or whose moves are not a legal game from all ones; T8 then loads its default state and shows the codec's own sentence (`LINK_NOTICE`). A board T8 does not offer is refused by the codec (rule 8, from the registry's `boards`). The codec has no hook for a tool's own validation; none was requested because this reading after `decodeState` gives the same result. Properties: `coords` other than numbers or a `start` other than all ones throw an authoring error naming the instance. Reason: instruction 5.2; trap "start other than all ones has no place in the graph".

## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: the graph, its edges and the stated limits
Decision: one `orbitGraph(board, [rho], 'firing', cap, undefined, true)` per board, cap `CAPS.weylExplorerGroup` (F4 completes with exactly 1152 items, tested), vertices keyed by the entries joined with commas (`arith.show`, natively for SafeInt and Big), layer = depth. Edges: every legal move applied once more to every item, drawn once, downward; `|W| n / 2` edges (tested). `buildGraph(board, cap?)` takes the cap so a test can force a cut: then only layers 0 to `completeLayers` are drawn and counted, the sentence of SPEC 5.5 "stopped after N positions; layers 0 to L are complete" (N the number of items, L `completeLayers`) is shown in `data-out="limit"` and in the figure's text alternative, and no path count is given. A `null` count reads "count not offered". T8 passes no `maxDepth` and takes no `layers` bound: 4.0 "Growth off the finite types" names T2 and T4, and every board T8 offers is finite, so "stopped after L layers" never arises; the `layers` property is accepted and ignored. T8 never writes "not of finite type" (tested over the component's source, the captions and the sentences). Reason: instruction 5.3.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: the edge rule above 192 elements and the canvas
Decision: at most 192 elements (D4 included, the boundary is strict) every edge is drawn, coloured by node through `var(--edge-k)` and carrying the node label as text; above 192 (A5, B4, C4, F4) only the edges of the current game and the edges at the focused vertex, both directions (into it from the layer above and out of it to the layer below), are drawn. `data-out="edgeMode"` reads `all` or `game-and-focus`. Above 192 the figure is a Canvas 2D drawing after hydration; the server render (and the no-JavaScript reader) gets the SVG snapshot that `svgScene.ts` writes from the same `Scene` (layout.ts) in Node, `data-out="snapshot"`, `role="img"` and an aria-label naming the board, the positions and the layers. After hydration the snapshot stays in place, hidden under the canvas once it is drawn, and is rewritten one task after each draw: it is the mirror of the drawn edges (`data-out="edge"`) and what ToolFrame's SVG and PNG export take (ToolFrame exports the figure's visible SVGs; a canvas cannot be exported, see requests). The canvas is wrapped in an element with `role="img"` and the same label (Svelte refuses `role="img"` on a canvas). Reason: instruction 5.7; 7.1; 7.4.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: no long task on F4

## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: geometry, hit areas, keyboard model and names
Decision: layout from `layeredLayout` (rank = number of moves). Hit areas are squares of 44 pixels when the widest layer has at most 6 vertices (A1, A2, A3, B2, C2, G2: 6 x 48 + 32 = 320), otherwise 24, with columns 4 pixels wider than the hit area and rows of 72 pixels (all edges) or 44 (above 192), so no two hit areas overlap; the box has a fixed height (the drawing's, at most 480 pixels) and scrolls in both directions (A-11). Keyboard (plan reading of A-1): at most 192 elements the vertices are real buttons in one group with one tab stop (roving tabindex, the current vertex first); above 192 one focus button outside the canvas moves to the focused vertex. Left and Right move within a layer in drawn order, Up and Down to the vertex of the previous or next layer nearest in x, Home and End to the top and the bottom vertex, Enter or Space jumps; the focused vertex is scrolled into view. Hover on the canvas is a hit test within the hit area. Accessible name: `Position 2, minus 3, 2, layer 3`, with `, on the game` for a vertex of the path. The details panel shows the vertex pointed at, else the current position, so it is never empty and holds the information itself (P2). Reason: instruction 5.10.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: the jump, announcements, highlight
Decision: activating a vertex (click, Enter, Space, or a table row's jump button) sets the moves to its shortest word from `wordTo` in labels; with `linkTo` the word is published to the Game Board with `publishToolState`. Moves on T8's board use the A-4 templates through T1's announce.ts unchanged (a move that ends the game adds `Game over after N moves.`; play to the end is one `Played N moves to the end. ...` sentence). Sentences without a template (plan): `Jumped to position 2, minus 3, 2 by 3 moves.`, `Loaded the play 1, 3, 2. Plays with this stack: 2.`, `Board B3: 48 positions in 10 layers.` (numbers from the engine). Hover or focus of a vertex sets the highlight to `{ kind: 'position', board, value }` (label order), hover of a separating root to `{ kind: 'root', ... }`, cleared on leave; a position highlighted by another tool of the page marks its vertex (`data-highlighted`). Reason: instruction 5.4, 5.10.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A6: linkTo
Decision: T8 follows the Game Board named by `linkTo` with state.ts's `followToolState` (P1-A2's mechanism, which T1 publishes to on every change and follows for jumps; no second mechanism). It shows no board, no step and no play control of its own; it draws the path only when the followed state is a legal game from all ones in the numbers on a board T8 offers (`followedGame`), and otherwise draws no path and says "The linked board's position is not in this graph: the graph holds the positions reached from all ones by legal play." in `data-out="linkStatus"`. No page or fixture route on the integration branch places a Game Board and a T8 linked, so the browser item `A3 with a linked T1 highlights the path` is reported as a deviation; the Vitest test `a linked T1's state drives the path` drives the link module directly (no DOM needed: nanostores in Node). Reason: instruction 5.8.
