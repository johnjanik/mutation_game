# Decisions of RT-FIX-P3-export-long (red team round 1: P3-RT-04-02, P3-RT-04-05, P3-RT-03-03)

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-export-long: a rational whose denominator is 1 is an integer in the data export
Decision: `jsonSafe` in `site/src/lib/export.ts` hands the numerator of a `{ num, den: 1n }` rational to the integer rule of SPEC 6.6, so it is a JSON number when safe and decimal text otherwise; a fraction stays `p/q`. This replaces the words "`{ num, den }` rationals become `p/q` or `p`" of the entry "2026-10-10 P1-A2: data export". What the specification left open: whether an integral rational counts among the "integers" of 6.6 (SPEC 5.3 types the form values and the d_i as rationals, and 5.7 rule 4 stores the golden `form_value` as text, but that rule governs `golden_data.json`, not the export). Default used: none of section 10; the reading of the round's orchestrator ("rationals with denominator 1 are integers") and of the entry "2026-10-10 P1-A5: Download data" (safe integers as numbers, fractions as `p/q`). Reason: P3-RT-04-02, confirmed by both verifiers.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-export-long: tools hand values, not display text, to the export
Decision: T3, T4 and T6 convert the exact texts they show back to export values at their `derivedForExport` boundary: an integer text by the integer rule (T3 and T6 through their existing `exportValue`, T4 through `integerOut` of `lib/state.ts`), a fraction text as `p/q`. T3's small values export a typed whole number as that integer and an unfinished entry (empty, or a lone minus sign) as its text, which is not an integer. The display texts and every hook are unchanged. Reason: P3-RT-04-05 and the T6 half of P3-RT-04-02 (SPEC 6.6).

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-export-long: existing expectations that recorded the old export
Decision: two assertions written before the round expected integers as text and were changed to the integer rule: `site/tests/unit/export.test.ts` (`{ num: -4n, den: 1n }` exports as `-4`, was `'-4'`) and `site/tests/e2e/T04.spec.ts` (the star's weights export as `1`, was `'1'`). Both stay exact comparisons. Reason: they encoded the behaviour the two findings report; no assertion was removed or loosened.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-export-long: T5's long dimension (SPEC 4.0 Long numbers)
Decision: above 12 digits `data-out="dimension"` shows T1's `shortValue` (`1.0000... x 10^120 (121 digits)`), with the exact digits in its `title` (the tooltip), in the table view (the M / D cell, now marked `data-table-exact="dimension"` beside the existing `D` and `M`) and in the export (unchanged, a decimal string). In the MathML formula a long dimension is the same shortened text in an `<mtext>` (it is not a numeral); up to 12 digits it stays an `<mn>`. The live region's sentence `Dimension N.` keeps the exact digits: the live region is visually hidden and the A-4 sentences speak exact values, so nothing is "shown" there. What the specification left open: how a formula and a spoken sentence carry a long number. Reason: P3-RT-03-03.
