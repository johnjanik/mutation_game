# RT-FIX-P3-follow-predict: decisions

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-follow-predict: a state loaded from outside withdraws an open prediction (P3-RT-13-03)
Decision: while an instance's prediction is still open, a state other than the configured one that reaches the instance from outside its own controls withdraws the prediction: the prediction block is no longer shown and the board is unfrozen, as for an instance without a prediction. A state equal to the configured one keeps the prediction open. In T1 the outside states are a link read on hydration, the back button, and a jump published by a linked tool (T2 or T8, through `followJumps`), all through `loadFromOutside` in `T01GameBoard.svelte`; in T3, T5, T9, T10, T11 and T15 they are a link and the back button (each tool's own `loadLink`, `applyLink` or `readLink`). What the specification left open: SPEC 4.0 Predict says the answer is computed by the engine and the board is frozen until the reader answers, and 6.6 that a link changes only what it names; neither says what happens when a link names another state for an instance with a prediction. The finding and both verifiers accepted either ignoring the link or dropping the prediction. Default used: none of section 10. Reason: a withdrawn prediction keeps what the link names (6.6, and "Copy link" after an answer and a move gives the reader's position to the next reader), while an ignored link would lose it; and no question then stands over a board it does not describe, nor shows its answer under an open question. T6, T7 and T8 also accept a prediction, but no instance on the site gives them one; their link code was not changed (an open question in the report).

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-follow-predict: a following garden holds the whole play (P3-RT-13-04)
Decision: whenever T2 (re)starts its follow model, the model covers the play the garden holds: its start and every position up to the cursor, with the edge of each move, ranked by the bounded orbitGraph of the play's start (`followPlay` in `t02/follow.ts`, the model that `followStart` and one `followStep` per move give). This holds for the first state a linked T1 publishes (a garden that hydrates when scrolled to after the board was played), any load (a link, the back button, a picked board, another board, coords or start of the followed T1), the switch to follow mode and the switch of what grows; a reset holds only the start, as before. This replaces, in the P1-A6 entry "follow mechanics and ranking", the words "the garden starts with the position the board stands on when follow begins". What the specification left open: nothing; SPEC 4 T2 ("vertices appear as the reader visits them on the linked T1"), 3 M1 row 1 (6 populations) and M4 row 5 (3 positions) count every position the board reached. Recorded because a builder's entry said otherwise.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-follow-predict: T8 follows a linked board by extending its game (sibling of P3-RT-04-04)
Decision: T8's subscription to `followToolState` is untracked, as T2's and T3's (it subscribed inside a tracked effect, whose immediate callback read and wrote the game, so the effect subscribed again after every move). Each published state with the same board, the numbers and the start all ones is applied in place to the game T8 holds (`followMoves` in `t08/link.ts`): the moves both share are kept, an undo or a redo moves the cursor, each new move is one legal firing; a move that is not a legal firing leaves the graph (the path is hidden, as before) and keeps the legal moves before it; another board, start or game is checked from the start with `followedGame` and loaded, as before. What the specification left open: nothing (SPEC 7.4 "extends it by one position per move, so a move costs one engine call however long the play is"). Reason: the orchestrator's note on P3-RT-04-04 asked whether T8 had the same cause; it had both parts of it.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-follow-predict: done controls keep the focus (P3-RT-05-09 and its siblings)
Decision: after hydration a control that its own press makes inactive keeps the focus: it carries `aria-disabled="true"` instead of the HTML `disabled` attribute, and its handler does nothing in that state. This covers Predict's Check and Show me once the prediction is answered (P3-RT-05-09, the remedy the board-a11y fixer recommended); T8's graph vertices and its focus button while T8 is frozen (as Board.svelte since P3-RT-05-02); T4's "keep counting" while it counts, and, when the count is in and the button goes, a focus left on it moves to the counter section (`tabindex="-1"`), inside the tool; and Step, Play to the end and Play on of T1, T5, T7, T8, T9 and T15 while a play runs in the worker, at the end of the game, or at a cap (the "controls disabled while busy" of the board-a11y fixer's open questions; the end state is the same cause, since a play to the end ends the game under the pressed control). The `disabled` attribute stays for the time before hydration (7.4) and while a prediction freezes a board, as the entry "2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-board-a11y: a frozen board stays one tab stop" records for History and the step controls; Predict's answer fields stay `disabled` once answered, since the answer is given from the two buttons and the focus is never in a field then. On the play controls `aria-disabled` follows the state alone, so it is in the server markup too when the default state is already at its end (T5's default play), and hydration still only removes `disabled`. With T4's change, the notice of a count that could not be continued (`data-out="counterNotice"`) is now also spoken in the frame's one polite live region (A-4; 4.0 "a polite live region announces each result"), beside "Counting in the background." What the specification left open: SPEC 7.5 A-1 (with WCAG 2.4.3 among its criteria) names no focus target after an action. Reason: P3-RT-05-09 and the orchestrator's note on its residuals.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-follow-predict: counting a follower's engine moves in the browser
Decision: the regression tests for a follower's cost per move (`site/tests/e2e/T02.spec.ts`, M1 and M2; `site/tests/e2e/T08.spec.ts`, M5) count the engine's moves made by one move of the leader through Chromium's precise coverage (`site/tests/e2e/engineMoves.ts`): the calls of the engine's `checkMove`, which every `mutate` and `fire` calls once, found in the bundle by its two error messages. They compare the count after a short and a long play, which must be equal (SPEC 7.4). Deviation: CONTRACTS 5.2 says Playwright tests read only the hooks of 4.0 and the live region; these tests read those hooks and, for this one measurement, the coverage counts of the page's scripts, as the timing tests read `performance.now()`. What the specification left open: how to check "a move costs one engine call however long the play is" on the built site. Reason: the orchestrator's note on P3-RT-04-04 (a partial revert of that fix passed every follower test, the timing test included); on a build with that revert the count is 602 against 3 (M1) and 603 against 4 (M2), on the fixed build 2 and 2.
