# Decisions of RT-FIX-P3-lesson-m0 (red team round 1, cluster lesson-m0)

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-lesson-m0: the underline and icon of a view word in lesson prose
Decision: in M0, every naming of the Populations or the Numbers view in prose is rendered by a local helper `View` (in `site/src/content/modules/m0-start.mdx`, beside `Term`) as `<span data-view-word="populations|numbers">`: the icon of `site/src/components/draw/palette.ts` (`POPULATIONS_ICON`, `NUMBERS_ICON`, the same icons as the panel titles of T1), 1em high, before the word, and a CSS text-decoration underline of 2px, offset 0.2em, coloured `var(--accent-populations)` or `var(--accent-numbers)`; the word itself keeps the text colour, and the icon is drawn with a stroke and no fill. What the specification left open: SPEC 8.3 [D] says the accents mark "the underlines of the two words" and that the icons are "used wherever the view is named", but not how the underline is drawn or where the icon sits. Default used: none of section 10 applies; the concrete form above is this fixer's. Reason: the finding P3-RT-13-08 reads `text-decoration-line` and `text-decoration-color` on the word and an icon in the same element; the home page (`index.astro`, P0-A5) draws its single view word with a `border-bottom` instead, which is the one other place with this mark. A border does not satisfy the reading of attack 27, so the lessons use the text-decoration underline.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-lesson-m0: request, one shared component for the view words
Decision: the helper lives in M0 only, because this cluster may edit `m0-start.mdx` and `M0.spec.ts` and nothing else. Request to MERGE (or the owner of `site/src/components/content`): move `View` into a shared content component with the same markup (`data-view-word`, icon from `palette.ts`, text-decoration underline in the accent) so that M4 and later lessons use one implementation; M0 then imports it and its regression test in `M0.spec.ts` stays as it is. What the specification left open: CONTRACTS.md 7 names no component for the view words. Default used: none. Reason: P3-RT-13-08 names M0 and M4; the M4 part belongs to another fixer this round, and two local copies would drift.

## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-lesson-m0: "finite type" and "highest root" in M0
Decision: the first "finite type" of M0 (beat 4) links `glossary#finite` and the next sentence says what the boards of finite type are, by the definition of SPEC 2.7 ("one of the boards of section 2.8"): "The boards of finite type form a short list, which contains A3; module three explains which boards belong to it." No characterisation (finitely many root populations, a positive definite form) is stated there, since each is a theorem that module three states with its provenance. The provenance sentence itself is unchanged: "verified by computation on every board of finite type of rank at most eight" is what `reference/verify.py` checks (`C.S_i = R_i.C` for every type of its `TYPES`, the finite types of rank at most eight). Its later uses in the Seen box link the glossary too. "Affine" in statement five links `glossary#affine`. "Highest root" is no longer used in M0: the caption of b5 and the explanation of its challenge describe the population (1, 1, 1) as one Martian in every city, because a definition there would need "root population", which module one introduces. The challenge recorded by P1-A8 (decisions/P1-A8.md, challenge 1) keeps its goal, its check and its board. What the specification left open: SPEC 8.4 asks for a definition at first use without fixing its wording. Default used: none. Reason: P3-RT-13-06.
