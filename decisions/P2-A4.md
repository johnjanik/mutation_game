## 2026-10-10 P2-A4: the history after a long play
Decision: `AffineGame` (t15/model.ts) caches positions: the start, every position reached by a hand move or a step, the end of a play (the `position` of `playFate`), and every position scrubbed to. A position is computed from the nearest cached index, forwards or backwards (each move is an involution), with `applyWord` on the main thread when the distance is at most `CAPS.mainThreadItems` (2000) and through `engineCall('applyWord', ...)` with `{ kind: 'play', moves: distance }` (the worker) otherwise. Undo is one move back from the cached current position and never calls `legalNodes` (unit test counts the calls). A link with more than 2000 moves is checked (`firstForbiddenMove`) and replayed in the worker before it is shown; up to 2000 on the main thread. `positionsOf` is never called. The strip is a slider with a counter above 50 positions (History.svelte), whose position names are computed only for the cursor. What the specification left open: how the strip of a play known only by its word and end is scrubbed (instruction 5.8). Default used: the plan's fallback mechanism.

## 2026-10-10 P2-A4: play on: routing, cap and report
Decision: `play on` is one call `engineCall('playFate', [board, a, undefined, cap], { board, kind: 'play', moves: CAPS.playMoves, finiteKnown: false })`; `whereToRun` answers `'worker'` for this hint on every offered board (unit test) and `engineCall` sends `playFate` to the worker anyway. It plays from the current position with `cap = CAPS.playMoves - cursor`, so the move list never exceeds 20000 moves and every state stays a link (6.6 rule 5); the report counts moves from the start of the move list, `cursor + moves` (and `cursor + firstVisit`), which at cursor 0 (every tested case, and the only case at the cap in practice) is exactly the engine's count, and gives `still running after 20000 moves` exactly when the list reaches the cap. Play on is disabled at 20000 moves. The report strings: `ended after k moves`, `returned to an earlier position after k moves`, `still running after 20000 moves`; at one move `ended after 1 move` (plan: singular). For a loop the tool adds `data-out="firstVisit"`: "The play came back to a position it had seen: the position repeated was first reached after j moves" (or "is the start"). What the specification left open: a play on from a later position. Reason: keeping every state a link (instruction 5.2) without lowering the cap of 5.6.
