## 2026-10-25 P6-tikz: T2 exports the garden as drawn, a tikz-graph
Decision: T02OrbitGarden.svelte passes ToolFrame's `latexForExport` a function, read at the click, that returns `gardenLatex(...)` of the new pure module `t02/latex.ts` for the garden as drawn now: `{ kind: 'tikz-graph', data: { nodes, edges, directed: false } }`. Nodes: every vertex drawn, in the order of the drawing (the order of `items`, of the follow model or of the positive view, whichever is shown), at the centre the layered layout of `t02/drawing.ts` gives it (`cx`, `cy`, SVG units, that is CSS pixels, y down), converted to centimetres at 96 CSS pixels to the inch with y negated (y up, so layer 0 is the top row of the picture as on screen), and scaled down uniformly when the drawing is wider than 16 cm (`MAX_WIDTH_CM`; plan: about the text width of an A4 page; only the width is bounded, as the request says, so a deep garden such as the triangle at 40 layers is tall). Label: when the drawing labels its vertices (at most 300 positions and the labels toggle on, SPEC 4 T2 Size), the population or numbers of the vertex in label order, every digit (4.0 Long numbers: no shortening in an export), entries joined by commas with no space and written with the ASCII hyphen-minus (never U+2212, which the screen shows and pdflatex cannot set in math mode); export.ts writes it in math mode. A vertex drawn as a dot (above 300 positions, or labels off) gets no label (`{}`), as on screen. Edges: the edges drawn now, in the drawn order, as vertex index pairs; above 300 positions these are the edges along the current play (pressing Export LaTeX moves the focus to the button, so the edges of a focused vertex are no longer drawn at the click). What the specification left open: everything beyond "plain TikZ coordinates" (6.7). Reason: the request ("the graph as currently drawn ... in the layered layout of t02/drawing.ts"); P6-A5's tikz-graph form.
