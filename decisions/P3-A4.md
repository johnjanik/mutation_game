## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: the reading of "the chambers"
Decision: the chambers are the |W| sectors (6, 8, 8, 12) cut out by the N mirror lines, one perpendicular to U q for each positive root q. All N lines are drawn (`data-out="mirror"`, `data-mirror` = 1-based index in `positiveRoots` order); the two simple mirrors are solid, thicker and labelled "mirror 1" and "mirror 2" (`data-simple`), the others dashed and thin. The dominant chamber (every simple root pairs positively with its bisector, 2.6) is filled with the neutral token at low opacity and labelled "dominant" in words (`data-dominant="true"`, on that chamber only). What the specification left open: what "the chambers" means in the drawing. Plan reading, flagged to the orchestrator.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: drawing conventions, the screen flip and the window
Decision: `choleskyBasis(board)` once per board; COLUMN j of U is the picture of alpha_(j+1). A root population q is drawn at U q, a position a at U C^{-1} a (`toPopulation`). Screen: an SVG box of 400 by 400 user units, the window [-R, R] squared filling it, screen y = -y so that alpha_2 is above the axis and the angle reads counterclockwise. R = 2 times the larger of the longest root and |U C^{-1}(1,1)|, rounded up to a half unit (A2: 3, B2 and C2: 4.5, G2: 7.5). Every coordinate in markup has 3 decimals, with -0.000 written 0.000. A point outside the window is not drawn; `data-out="offView"` says so in words. What the specification left open: the window and the scale. Plan.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: the snapping rule
Decision: on release of a drag, the real numbers a = C U^{-1} y of the pointer position are rounded and the 3 by 3 neighbourhood of the rounded pair is searched for the nearest weight-lattice point (U C^{-1} a'); ties (within 1e-12) go to the smaller a_1, then the smaller a_2. Pointer capture, release on pointerup, pointercancel and lostpointercapture; `touch-action: none` on the point's handle only (a 22-unit transparent circle), so the page scrolls elsewhere. Plan (5.6).

## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: the overlays property and boardChoices
Decision: property `overlays: ('roots' | 'orbit' | 'lattices')[]`, default `[]`, sets the initial state of the three switches; the switches are not part of a link (6.6, schema 1). Property `boardChoices: string[]` limits the picker (default the four boards). Root-lattice dots are small filled discs, weight-lattice dots open diamonds, so on G2 every dot visibly carries both markers. Roots are drawn as arrows from the origin; a root equal to the page's `highlight` (kind `root`, this board, value in label order) is drawn thicker in the focus colour and carries `data-marked`. T6 does not set the highlight (optional in the plan).

## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: SafeInt overflow (5.8)
Decision: positions are kept as bigint; each engine call uses the SafeInt board while every entry is safe and its `bigBoard` copy otherwise (and retries with Big on `EngineOverflow`), so a 40-digit link replays exactly and never crashes the island. `choleskyBasis` is always called on the SafeInt board (the floats of the drawing do not depend on the arithmetic). Plan reading by analogy with SPEC 4 T1.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: the line a = Cp in plain markup (deviation from A-12)
Decision: the line is built as 7.3 prescribes for mathematics produced while a tool runs: C as an HTML table with a caption and row and column headers, the population entries through `Frac.svelte`, the numbers as a vector of spans, the whole line in words as the `aria-label` of its `role="group"`. This is table markup, not MathML, so it does not meet the letter of A-12; deviation flagged. If P3-A5 reports that native MathML renders in all three engines, the line can move to MathML later.

## 2026-10-10 P3-A4: board names in plain markup, not names.json (deviation)
Decision: the board name is set as the letter with the rank in `<sub>` (`data-name` holds `A2`), and the picker's `<option>`s are plain text. names.json is not imported. Reason: the precedent of P1-A6 and P2-A3 (MERGE accepted): an `<option>` cannot hold KaTeX HTML, and names.json would ship all 32 entries and need KaTeX's stylesheet for four names.
