## 2026-10-25 P6-terms: the shared spec glossary-later-uses.spec.ts
Decision: `site/tests/e2e/glossary-later-uses.spec.ts` applies the rule of `M8.spec.ts > later uses of a term link to the glossary` (SPEC 8.4: "Define every term on first use and link later uses to a glossary entry") to the lessons M0 to M9 and to history, confusions, open-problem, not-quiver-mutation and about: for every glossary term (each `dfn[id]` of the glossary, formula left out) used at least twice in the prose of a page, some use after the first is a link to the term's own anchor, and every glossary link of a term resolves. The prose scope is M8's: the article without tools, tables, the list of sources, the module links, challenges, h1 to h3, scripts and formulas. A use is the term as a whole word, any case, singular as the glossary writes it. Three refinements that M8's test does not need, each so that the rule asks only for links that can exist and mean what they say: (1) a hyphen joins a word on either side (M8's test stops a term only at a following hyphen, so there "anti-Martians" holds a use of "Martians" and "non-compact" one of "compact"); (2) a use inside the use of a longer term belongs to the longer term ("restricted board" is not a use of "board"); (3) the text of a link that is not a glossary link (a module title in the Developed line of M0, a statement of the about page linked to its module, a source) is not a use, because links do not nest. A link to the glossary page without a fragment is navigation, not a term link. Reason: SPEC 8.4 and MERGE's instruction (step 3).
