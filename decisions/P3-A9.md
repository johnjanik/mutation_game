## 2026-10-10 P3-A9: the Lie theory paragraph
Decision: the graph of T8 is named the Hasse diagram of the left weak order: a move multiplies the element reached on the left (the play i_1, ..., i_k reaches w = s_{i_k} ... s_{i_1}, and the next move gives s_i w), and the separating roots of the position w rho are the positive roots made negative by w^{-1}, its inversion set ([Hum90] 1.7; checked on the example 1, 3, 2, whose roots (0,1,0), (0,1,1), (1,1,0) are those of the reduced word 1, 3, 2 of w^{-1}). The wave with class 0 fired first is the product ... c_1 c_0 of h factors, a power (c_1 c_0)^{h/2} only for even h. Reason: instruction 5.12.
