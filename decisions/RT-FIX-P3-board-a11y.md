## 2026-10-17 RT-FIX-P3-board-a11y: a frozen board stays one tab stop (P3-RT-05-02)
Decision: after hydration the `disabled` prop of `Board.svelte` no longer sets the HTML `disabled` attribute on the node buttons; it sets `aria-disabled="true"`, and activating a node calls no `onAct`. The roving tab stop, the arrow keys, the A-3 names (`cannot be fired` and the city states) and `data-can-act="false"` stay as they were, so a reader on the keyboard reaches the board and its values while the prediction waits, and no move is made. Before hydration the buttons are still rendered with `disabled` (7.4). The same holds while a tool passes `disabled` for a running play (`busy` in T1 and T9). What the specification left open: what "frozen" means for the board (4.0 Predict); A-1 and 4.0 "Each board is one tab stop" fix that the board keeps its tab stop. This replaces the words "and disabled" in the entry "2026-10-10 P1-A5: prediction check" for the node buttons; History and the step controls stay disabled while frozen. Reason: SPEC 7.5 A-1, 4.0 Keyboard and screen reader, 4.0 Predict, 7.4. No prop or hook name changed (CONTRACTS 5.1).
