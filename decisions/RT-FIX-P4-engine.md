# RT-FIX-P4-engine: decisions of the phase 4 round fixer for the engine cluster

## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-engine: wythoffEdges past 2^53 on a Big board throws EngineOverflow (P4-RT-01-01 P4-RT-03-01)
Decision: `wythoffEdges` throws `EngineOverflow` when an edge it is about to return has an entry that is not a safe integer, on every arithmetic, instead of returning `Number()` of the bigint. The check is made when the edge is recorded, after the cap test, so a search that the cap stops before such an edge returns its items as before; on `SafeInt` the moves themselves throw, as they always did, and may do so earlier because `SafeInt` checks every product (`G2` with the ring 3002399751580330 on its long node: exact edges on `Big`, `EngineOverflow` on `SafeInt`). What the specification left open: SPEC 5.4 lists `wythoffEdges` as exact and E-2 gives `Big` exact values, while CONTRACTS.md 2.4 phase 4 types the items as `[number[], number[]]`, which cannot hold an entry beyond 2^53; decisions/P4-A1.md ("entries converted to number") decides the order and truncation only. Default used (SPEC section 10): none. Reason: E-10 names `EngineOverflow` for a value the result cannot hold, `SafeInt` already throws it on the same input, and the recorded rule of `symbolicPlay` (decisions/P3-A2.md "the overflow rule of symbolicPlay") is the same reading of a `number[]` contract; returning bigint entries would change the signature of CONTRACTS.md 2.4. Both reproducers accept the throw.

## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-engine: symmetrizer and gram read the entries through bigint (P4-RT-01-02)
Decision: `symmetrizer` and `gram` build the rationals of the Cartan entries as `rat(BigInt(x))` from the integer copy of the matrix, not `rat(x)`. The rule of decisions/P1-A1.md that a number argument of `rat` must be a safe integer is kept unchanged; the entries of a board are integers of any size (SPEC 5.6, first table: "the engine accepts any count"; `fromAdjacency` accepts any integer), so they go into bigint before `rat` sees them. What the specification left open: nothing about the values (the oracle and SPEC 5.6 fix them); this records why `rat` itself was not changed. Default used (SPEC section 10): none. Reason: the smallest change at the cause; `cartanClass`, `classify`, `smallestNonfiniteSubboard` and every guarded function reach the rationals only through `symmetrizer` (in `classOfConnected`) and `gram`, and the elimination of `classByMinors` already moves to bigint when a value leaves the safe range.

## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-engine: the hash of the orbit fast path and the export positionHash (P4-RT-03-02)
Decision: `searchOnNumbers` (search.ts) hashes a position as the sum, mod 2^30, of one share per entry, the share being the low 32 bits of the entry and the rest (entry = rest times 2^32 plus low) mixed with the position's odd seed by the finaliser of MurmurHash3 (two multiplications and three shifts). The sum keeps the update in the entries a move changes; the mix makes the share not linear in the entry, so a sublattice such as 2^30 Z^n no longer falls into one bucket. The positions the hash picks are still compared entry by entry, so every result, order and cap is the generic loop's, as decisions/RT-FIX-P3-residual-perf.md requires. `positionHash` is exported from search.ts (not from index.ts, so not part of the public API of CONTRACTS.md 2.4) for the counting test. What the specification left open: SPEC 5.5 keys the orbit hash set by the decimal text and says nothing on a faster equivalent; the residual fix recorded the method only as "a hash of the entries". Default used (SPEC section 10): none. Reason: the instruction of the round asks for a multiplicative mix per entry with a non-linear finaliser that keeps every result, order and cap. Measured on this machine (load 7 to 13 on 4 processors): E8, firing, from 2^30 (1, ..., 1), cap 40000: one hash for all 40000 positions and 799980000 comparisons before, 39999 hashes and 1 comparison after; the fast path took 13.6 to 19.6 s before and 0.05 to 0.22 s after (seeds 2^29 to 2^40 times (1, ..., 1)) against 0.6 to 0.9 s for the generic loop. Warm, interleaved in one process: the orbit benchmark's search (12-cycle with a chord, 2000 positions, Big) 1.94 ms before and 1.60 ms after, the T4 load path 11.7 ms before and 11.8 to 13.1 ms after; E8, firing, from (1, ..., 1) at 100000 positions 619 ms before and 254 ms after.

## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P4-engine: what the orbit benchmark measures, and request for its bigint case (P4-RT-11-02, needs-decision)
Decision: record and request; packages/engine/test/bench/orbit.bench.ts is not changed on this branch. Record: since the residual fix of P3-RT-07-02 (decisions/RT-FIX-P3-residual-perf.md item 4), `orbitGraph` on a `Big` board searches on plain numbers while every value is a safe integer, so the benchmark of SPEC 7.6 (the 12-cycle with the chord 1 to 7, a `Big` board, seeds 0 and 1, cap 2000, 40 layers, median of seven, 25 ms) times the search on numbers and no longer the bigint loop with decimal keys that it timed at G3; no entry recorded this until now. What the specification left open: SPEC 7.6 says "2000 positions on a board with 12 nodes in `Big` arithmetic", which can name the board (E-2: a board carries its arithmetic) or the computation timed (5.5 and `reference/bench_orbit.mjs` time bigint). Default used (SPEC section 10): none. The fix asked for this round (a second case on the same board with the seeds times 2^60, the same 2000 positions in the bigint loop, also against 25 ms) was written and it catches the lens's mutant F9 (bigint case median 588.54 ms, the first case 21.27 ms and passing), but on this machine the bigint case does not stay under 25 ms: medians at a one-minute load of about 2 (the quietest seen in about 50 minutes of waiting; the five-minute load was 4.6): 31.84, 24.29 and 30.02 ms (the first case 20.30, 13.91, 10.68 ms); at about 4.5: 29.12, 22.81, 21.60 ms; at about 10: 22.92, 34.23, 40.47 ms; at 11 to 12: 26.58 to 36.68 ms; under `nice -n -20` at 15: 37.34, 24.82, 30.80 ms. For scale, P3-A2 measured this container at about 2.3 times slower than the processor the 25 ms line was sized for, and the bigint loop with seeds 0 and 1 measured 19.75 to 21.60 ms alone at G3 (MERGE P3 stage 3.1; GATE G3 21.01 ms); the RT-11 lens measured the times-2^60 case at 21.68 ms at load 6. In one process at a load of about 2, alternating the engine of the base 967118d and of this branch, the times-2^60 search took a median of 19.93 and 20.98 ms over 30 runs (23.26 and 24.89 over the first seven) on the base and 19.45 and 20.49 ms (21.23 and 22.87) on this branch: the bigint loop is unchanged by this branch and sits just under the line warm. A threshold is not the fixer's to change, so the case is not committed and the branch stays green. Options: (a) keep 25 ms for both cases and make the bigint loop faster first (for example a key or hash built only from the entries a move changes, as the numbers path does), then add the case; (b) the owner reads "in `Big` arithmetic" as naming the board, records that the benchmark times the numbers path, and adds the bigint case with a line the owner sets for this container; (c) add the case as written and let GATE measure it alone on a quiet machine, accepting that it may fail there. Recommendation: (a), since the bigint loop is what the main thread runs on a custom or affine board once a value passes 2^53, and 7.6 sizes the 25 ms line for that case. Request, for whoever makes the change after the decision: in packages/engine/test/bench/orbit.bench.ts, after the closing `});` of the test "12-cycle with a chord: 2000 positions in Big within 25 ms", add a test "12-cycle with a chord: the same 2000 positions with the seeds times 2^60 (the bigint loop) within 25 ms" with the comment `// regression: P4-RT-11-02` that builds the same board, checks `board.arith` is `Big`, makes the twelve unit seeds and the same seeds times `2n ** 60n`, checks once that the scaled search has 2000 items, truncated true, the same `depth`, `parent` and `node` as the unit search and every item 2^60 times the unit item, then times seven runs of `orbitGraph(b, scaled, 'mutation', 2000, 40)`, writes `orbit benchmark, bigint loop (seeds times 2^60): median ... ms` with `process.stdout.write`, and expects the median to be at most 25 ms; this is the lens's packages/engine/test/bench/redteam-RT-11-big-values.bench.ts on branch worktree-agent-a19febfb0f4c5a241 folded into the benchmark file, with a header note that the first case times the numbers path and the second the bigint loop.
