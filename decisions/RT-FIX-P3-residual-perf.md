## 2026-10-24 RT-FIX-P3-residual-perf: four engine functions made faster without changing a result
Decision: (1) cartanClass decides on the leading principal minors of C instead of those of B = DC: D is diagonal with positive entries, so the k-th minor of B is d_1...d_k times that of C and has its sign; the minors are the pivots of a fraction-free (Bareiss) elimination on numbers, on bigint when a product leaves the safe range; the balance test is unchanged (symmetrizer). (2) smallestNonfiniteSubboard tests connectedness of the 2^n - 1 subsets on bit masks (n at most 30, the old walk above) and applies that rule to the induced matrix without building a sub-board. (3) symmetrizer writes all ones on a symmetric matrix (the walk gives exactly that). (4) orbitGraph on a SafeInt or Big board searches on plain numbers while every value is a safe integer, finding earlier positions by a hash of the entries and comparing them, skipping the two moves that can never give a new position (a node whose number is 0, and the move that found the position); when a value would leave the safe range the items found so far are written back with their keys and the generic loop goes on from that move (bigint on Big; on SafeInt that move throws EngineOverflow as before). What the specification left open: how the engine computes these results; SPEC 5.5, 5.6 and the oracle fix only the results, the order of search and of candidates, and the caps. Default used: none. Reason: SPEC 7.6 and E-9 on main-thread work. Every result, order, cap and interface is unchanged: the golden suite passes, and packages/engine/test/unit/mainthread-fast-paths.test.ts checks each function against the oracle rule it replaces (cartan_class through the minors of B, smallest_nonfinite_subboard, the generic search loop with decimal keys) on random boards, on values past 2^53 and on SafeInt overflow (same EngineOverflow message at the same move).
