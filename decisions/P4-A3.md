## 2026-10-17 P4-A3: the largest ring
Decision: a ring is an integer from 0 to `RING_MAX` = 10^6 - 1 (six digits, `CAPS.formFinderDigits`, the cap of an entry typed into T3); the ring buttons, the number fields of the table view and links are held to it. What the specification left open: SPEC says "a non-negative integer on each node". Reason: no orbit of the 32 boards then leaves `SafeInt` (the largest root coefficient is 6), so no link can make the engine throw `EngineOverflow`; the shape depends only on which nodes are ringed. Every value from 0 to `RING_MAX` is reachable without a mouse (the two ring buttons and the number fields).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A3: make uniform
Decision: the constant c is the largest ringed integer, so the picture keeps its size; the option is off by default and is not a link field. It is disabled with the sentence "Equal numbers on nodes of one length are uniform already." exactly when every ringed node has the same d_i and the same integer (`uniformMatters`; one ringed node; B3 (1,1,0)); with equal d_i and different integers it still acts (it makes them equal). The replay runs along the tree of `orbitGraph`: vertex k is `applyWordFloat` of its parent's real position by the one move that reached it, the same sequence of float operations as replaying `wordTo(graph, k)` from the start (unit test compares the two exactly). The lengths shown are measured on the drawn coordinates y = E C^-1 a and agree with 2 d_i a_i^2 within 1e-9. What the specification left open: c. Default used: the plan's (5.7).

## 2026-10-17 P4-A3: the move graph and its labels
Decision: the move graph joins mu to R_i mu for every node i with mu_i != 0, derived from the vertex list "once more" (5.4) by `t10/graphs.ts` with the engine's `fire`, deduplicated as sorted index pairs; for a preset it is read from the file's `moveAdjacency` (each pair's node found again with `fire`). Every drawn move-graph edge carries the label of its node as text, at every size (no threshold), on the canvas, in the SVG of the view and so in Export SVG and PNG: a numeral of 10 CSS pixels at the midpoint, in the text colour on a 3-pixel halo of the page colour (4.5 to 1 for numerals, A-6), drawn after the edges and points; the edge colour is `--edge-k` of the label (palette.ts `edgeColour`, 6.3); the depth cue acts on the edge width only (opacity 1), so no edge colour falls below its 3 to 1 against the page. A legend lists colour and label, and the table view lists each edge with its node (graphs of at most 300 edges). Where two edges cross at their midpoints (the two diagonals of the hexagon of A2) their labels overlap; nothing was thinned. Deviation from the plan's routing: the derivation is not sent to the worker above 2000 moves (the worker dispatches engine functions only, and no engine function computes a move graph); it runs on the main thread in slices of at most 2000 moves, one task each.
