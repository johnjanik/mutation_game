## 1. Objective

When you are done the site has tool T3, the Form Finder (id `form`, route `/tools/form`): a populations board in free play with a **meter** that shows the form value $(p,p)$ of the current population and never moves; a **builder** in which the reader sets a weight on every city and on every joined pair and presses "Test" to ask the engine whether every mutation preserves the resulting quadratic form, with the engine's witness shown when it does not; and **small values**, where the reader types populations of at most six digits per entry and reads each one's form value and whether it is a root population. The tool exists so that M2 ("The form behind the game", phase 2) can show the conserved quantity, let the reader discover that the recipe for the form is forced, and test the norm-two characterisation F2; M3 beat 2 places its small values beside T4, and M6 beat 3 (phase 4) uses its builder on $B_2$. Every acceptance criterion of SPEC 4 T3 becomes an automated test, which gate G2 requires.

### 5.4 The builder (SPEC 4 T3 "Builder"; 2.5; M2 beat 2; M6 beat 3)

- Quoted: "the reader sets a weight $w_i$ on each city and a weight $w_{ij}$ on each joined pair, which defines
  $$Q_w(p)=\sum_i w_i\,p_i^2-\sum_{i<j} w_{ij}\,p_ip_j .$$
  City weights are positive; road weights are zero or positive." One control per city (label order) and one per joined pair (2.1: joined when at least one road runs between them in either direction, so one weight per pair also on one-way boards and on pairs with $A_{ij}A_{ji}\ge4$). The city control cannot be set to 0 or below; the road control cannot be set below 0. Weights are integers (plan: the oracle's check used integers; the test is invariant under scaling, so integers lose nothing; the ranges must admit the passing weights of every named type, which are at most 6 on $G_2$; a slider with a paired number field for each weight is the plan's suggestion, since M2 asks for sliders and Playwright cannot `fill` a range input).
- "'Test' asks the engine (`formIsInvariant`) whether every mutation preserves $Q_w$ and, if not, shows the population and the mutation that the engine returns as a witness." The button is labelled exactly `Test`. Nothing is tested on page load or on a weight change by itself; the reader presses the button (4.0: nothing acts by itself; P9).
- The matrix handed to `formIsInvariant(board, M)`: $Q_w(p)=p^{\mathsf T}Mp$ with $M$ symmetric, $M_{ii}=w_i$ and $M_{ij}=M_{ji}=-w_{ij}/2$, because the cross term of $p^{\mathsf T}Mp$ is $2M_{ij}p_ip_j$. The oracle's check (verify.py 1527) builds exactly this matrix with `Fr(-wij, 2)` off the diagonal. Invariance is unchanged by scaling, so hand the engine the integer matrix $2M$ (diagonal $2w_i$, off-diagonal $-w_{ij}$) and avoid halves (plan). One convention throughout (plan): `matrixOf` returns $2M$ and that is the matrix passed to `formIsInvariant`; with the passing weights $2M=2B$, twice `gram(board)`. $M$ must be symmetric: `form_is_invariant` tests $S_i^{\mathsf T}MS_i=M$ for every node, while `form_invariance_witness` tests the quadratic form on test populations; on a non-symmetric matrix the first can fail where the second finds no witness, and the verdict would say "fails" with nothing to show (section 9).
- Verdict: when `invariant` is true the builder says so (plan wording, for example `passes: every mutation preserves this form`); when false it shows `witness.node` as a city label (`toLabels(board, [witness.node])[0]`) and `witness.population` in label order, and the population after that mutation from `mutate(board, population, witness.node)` so the reader sees the two populations whose values differ (plan). The oracle's witness is "a single Martian or two Martians in different cities" (`form_invariance_witness` docstring), found with the node outermost in increasing position and the test populations in the order unit vectors then pairs $(a,b)$ with $a<b$: the engine returns the first such pair. You may not compute $Q_w$ of the two populations in the component (7.3); no engine function evaluates a reader's form, so show the populations and the city, not the values. The verdict and the witness are `data-out="testVerdict"` and `data-out="witness"` (plan names) and the verdict is announced in the polite live region (plan).
- The passing weights (SPEC 4 T3, 2.5): "On a balanced connected board exactly one choice up to scale passes: $w_i=2d_i$ and $w_{ij}=2d_iA_{ji}$ (section 2.5)." Here $A_{ji}$ is the number of roads from $j$ to $i$, that is $-C_{ij}$, and the balance condition $d_iA_{ji}=d_jA_{ij}$ makes the road weight symmetric. With these weights $M=B=DC$, the engine's `gram`. On $A_2$: $d=(1,1)$, weights $(2,2)$ and road $2$: "passes exactly when all three are equal" (M2). On $B_2$: $C=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-2&2\end{pmatrix}$, $d=(2,1)$, city weights $4$ and $2$, road weight $2\cdot2\cdot1=2\cdot1\cdot2=4$: "passes exactly for city weights in the ratio $2:1$" (M6). On $G_2$: $d=(1,3)$, city weights $2$ and $6$, road weight $6$. On $B_3$ ($C_{32}=-2$): $d=(2,2,1)$, city weights $(4,4,2)$, road weights $4$ between 1 and 2 and $4$ between 2 and 3, the latter because $A_{23}=-C_{32}=2$ and $A_{32}=-C_{23}=1$ give $2d_2A_{32}=2\cdot2\cdot1=4=2d_3A_{23}=2\cdot1\cdot2$. On $F_4$: $d=(2,2,1,1)$, city weights $(4,4,2,2)$, road weights $4$, $4$, $2$. Check every one of these against `symmetrizer` in your test rather than typing them.
- "On a connected bidirected board that is not balanced, and on a board with a one-way road, no choice with positive city weights passes [V on two unbalanced triangles, 100 random choices each]." The builder works on such boards (5.6: `formIsInvariant` "works" on a one-way board) and reports a witness for every choice. The strictly positive city weights are what make this true: on the one-way road `[[2, 0], [-1, 2]]` the degenerate form `[[1, 0], [0, 0]]` is invariant (verify.py 1530), so a control that allowed 0 would let a reader "pass" with a form that is not one.
- Default weights (plan, record it): every city weight 1 and every road weight 1 unless the page passes initial weights. On a simply laced board this choice already passes (it is the Tits form up to scale), which the caption may state; on $B_2$ it fails, which M6 uses.

### 5.5 Small values (SPEC 4 T3 "Small values"; 2.7 F2; 2.11; 5.4; 5.5; 5.6; M2 beat 5)

## 9. Traps

- Road weight $w_{ij}=2d_iA_{ji}$, with $A_{ji}$ the roads from $j$ into $i$, equal to $-C_{ij}$. On $B_2$ the city weights are in the ratio $2:1$ (node 1 long, $d=(2,1)$), and the road weight equals the larger city weight, 4. The balance condition makes $d_iA_{ji}=d_jA_{ij}$, so the pair's weight is well defined; if your two expressions differ on a named type you have transposed $C$ or confused $A_{ij}$ with $A_{ji}$.
- Entries of at most six digits: the field refuses more (5.6). Count digits, not characters; refuse the seventh digit on typing and on paste; keep the sign out of the count (plan). The value `CAPS.formFinderDigits`, never a literal 6.
- The quadratic form and the matrix: $Q_w$ has the cross term $-w_{ij}p_ip_j$ once, $p^{\mathsf T}Mp$ has it twice, so $M_{ij}=-w_{ij}/2$ (verify.py 1527 builds `Fr(-wij, 2)`). Hand the engine the doubled integer matrix.
