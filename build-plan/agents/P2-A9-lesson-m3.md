## 9. Traps

- The statement "the list is A, D, E" is for simple graphs; $B$, $C$, $F$, $G$ have positive definite forms too (Appendix F last list; roster). Never write "positive definite exactly for $A$, $D$, $E$".
- Wildberger's List A against Kac's tables is [U] (E14): do not say they are the same list (roster). List A has seventeen entries [S]; that it matches Kac's sixteen with the star entered separately is [U].
- [W20] Theorem 8.8 is for balanced boards; Theorem 4.1 is for connected bidirected multigraphs without loops. Do not state the equivalences for one-way boards, where the theory does not apply and the root set of the single one-way road is infinite.
- Only `classify` decides finite type. A counter that stops at 40 layers or 2000 positions says so in the words of 4.0 and proves nothing (4.0 Stated limits; 5.6 last sentence). A caption must not say "not of finite type" from a cap.
- Labels on the star with four arms differ by source: `D4~` (T15, T1, T2, `affine.D4`, `kostant`) has labels 0 to 4 with the centre at node 2, marks `[1, 1, 2, 1, 1]`; `layer_growth_examples[label=star with four arms]` uses the same matrix (centre at position 2), but loaded as a custom matrix its labels are 1 to 5 and the centre is node 3; the finite board `D4` has labels 1 to 4 with the centre at node 2. The square is `square` in T15 (labels 1 to 4 clockwise from the top) and `A3~` elsewhere (labels 0 to 3). Never map one numbering onto another by hand; $(2;1,1,1,1)$ is notation with the centre first.
- Kostant's game takes the lowest unhappy city first (oracle `raising_play`, `unhappy[0]`); the path of A.4 from city 1 is that choice. The end, length and final numbers are the same from every city on $D_4$.
- `smallest_nonfinite_subboard` returns the first set in lexicographic order among the smallest (oracle docstring). On the star with five arms it is nodes 1 to 5 (the centre and the first four arms); do not say "the" smallest sub-board as if unique.
- The frozen population is not a root population (the root test returns false on it; it is fixed by every mutation). One Martian plus it is. Keep the two apart in prose and in T3's reading.
- "On the boards of this lesson any city will do" is [V] for untwisted affine boards only; on twisted boards some cities need $2\delta$ or $3\delta$ (2.12 table). Do not cite [W20] for "every city".
- The bidirected one-sign statement rests on Kac (E4); the one-way counterexample is [V] and the interpretation is [D]. Wrap the first; state the second; say the third is the site's reading.
- Smith's theorem citation is [U] (E10); [BrH] section 3.1.1 is [S] for the section title only.
