### 5.6 Symmetric play and the same numbers (T11; 2.13(a); E-5; 4.0 History; A-4)

- Quoted: "in 'symmetric play' one click acts on a whole orbit; the folded board shows the same numbers." A click, Enter or Space on any node of an orbit on the big board, or on the orbit's node on the folded board, makes one symmetric move. 2.13: "Acting on all nodes of an orbit, in any order, is a symmetric move"; expand in increasing position (E-5) and note that the order is immaterial only because the orbit's nodes are unjoined (property 4 of 5.7), which is exactly the condition under which the board folds.
- The theorem, quoted: "symmetric play of the mutation game on the big board is the mutation game on the folded board, and symmetric play of the numbers game on the big board is the numbers game on the folded board, with the same numbers." The folded board's values are computed by the engine on the folded board (`applyWord(folded, ...)`), not copied from the big board (plan), so the equality the reader sees is the theorem, and the unit test checks it on 200 random plays per row. Worked values for your tests, from the oracle: on $A_3$ with the flip from all ones in the numbers view, one symmetric move on the orbit of node 1 gives `(-1, 3, -1)` on the big board and `(-1, 3)` on the folded $B_2$; on $D_4$ with the rotation `3.2.4.1` from all ones, one symmetric move on the orbit of node 1 gives `(-1, 4, -1, -1)` and the folded $G_2$ shows `-1` on its node 2 and `4` on its node 1; on $A_3$ in the populations view from `(1, 0, 1)`, mutating the orbit of city 1 gives `(-1, 0, -1)` and `(-1, 0)`.

### 5.12 Performance (E-9; 7.6)

- E-9 decides before each call where it runs; T11 asks `whereToRun` (CONTRACTS 4 `worker.ts`) and never decides by itself. On the main thread: `symmetries` on at most 8 nodes, `fold`, `identify`, `averagedRootsType` (the roots of a finite board, known in advance, 5.7), and every play of at most 2000 moves.
- The worker, quoted from E-9 item 2: "a play or a word of more than 2000 moves: `playFate`, a play to the end on a board that is not of finite type, a long list of moves read from a link." A link may carry up to 20000 entries in `w` (6.6: "more than 20000 moves" is rejected), and in T11 each entry is a symmetric move that expands to its whole orbit (up to 3 nodes, the rotation of $D_4$), so the big board's expanded word can reach 60000 single moves. When `replay` reads the state on hydration it builds two hints (plan, built in `t11/model.ts` as `playHint(board, wordLength)`): `{ board, kind: 'play', moves: <length of the expanded word> }` for the big board and `{ board: folded, kind: 'play', moves: <number of symmetric moves> }` for the folded board. When `whereToRun` returns `'worker'`, the `applyWord` call goes through `engineCall('applyWord', args, hint)`; otherwise it runs on the main thread. A call is never started in one place and continued in the other (E-9).
- While a worker call is pending, both boards keep the start drawn, every node has `data-can-act="false"`, the controls stay disabled, and the live region says nothing (plan); when the result arrives the boards show the final positions and the controls are enabled. Moves the reader makes after that are applied to the last computed positions, one symmetric move at a time, on the main thread (plan: the History strip keeps the positions it has computed rather than replaying the whole word after each click). Errors from the worker are re-thrown as the same class (CONTRACTS 4) and are not caught silently.
- The default state has `moves: []`, so no default needs the worker (7.6: "No tool may need it for its default state"), and the worker's script is fetched only when a link with a long play is opened. Report the hints you pass under `workerCalls` (section 10).
- `/tools/fold` fetches at most 90 kB of gzipped JavaScript before the first interaction (7.6); import from `@engine` only what you use, so the tool's own share stays near the 30 kB the table allots. GATE measures with `npm run size`; you do not run it.
