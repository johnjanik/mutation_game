Beat by beat, the ideas each paragraph must carry (SPEC 3 M7 and 2.14):

1. **Change of coordinates.** Populations are integer vectors with the invariant form, which is positive definite but not the dot product. To draw, convert to coordinates in which the form is the dot product: $y = E\,p$ with $E^{\mathsf T}E = B$, so that $y\cdot y' = (p,p')$ (quote the formula through `<Tex>`; the recipe $G = D^{1/2}CD^{-1/2}$, $G = V\Lambda V^{\mathsf T}$, $E = \Lambda^{1/2}V^{\mathsf T}D^{1/2}$ may be given in one sentence or left to the Lie theory paragraph). A position of the numbers game is drawn at $y = E\,C^{-1}a$. Any other factorisation of $B$ gives the same picture rotated (the kaleidoscope of M4 used the triangular one). Floating point enters only here; everything before is exact. Figures: the hexagon of $A_2$, the 12 roots of $A_3$ as a cuboctahedron, the 24 roots of $D_4$ as a 24-cell in projection. Provenance: [V for every type of rank 2 to 8; oracle `euclidean_basis`].
2. **Wythoff's construction on the board.** Put a positive number on some nodes ("ring" them, Appendix C), zero on the rest, take the orbit under free play of the numbers game: its convex hull is a polytope whose combinatorics depend only on which nodes are ringed. Vertices: $|W|/|W_U|$ of them, $U$ the unringed nodes. From $A_3$: tetrahedron $(1,0,0)$, octahedron $(0,1,0)$, cuboctahedron $(1,0,1)$, truncated tetrahedron $(1,1,0)$, truncated octahedron $(1,1,1)$; from $B_3$: octahedron $(1,0,0)$, cube $(0,0,1)$, cuboctahedron $(0,1,0)$, truncated octahedron $(1,1,0)$, truncated cube $(0,1,1)$, rhombicuboctahedron $(1,0,1)$, truncated cuboctahedron $(1,1,1)$ (the table of 2.14, every face count through `<G>` on `wythoff[type=..;rings=..].f_vector`). Provenance: Wythoff's construction in the language of weights, [Cox35], [CKPS95], both [S2]: cite by key, state nothing about what either paper says beyond that.
3. **Counting faces from the board alone.** A face type is a set $K$ of nodes such that every connected component of $K$ contains a ringed node; its faces have dimension $|K|$, each is the orbit polytope of the sub-board $K$, and their number is $|W| / (|W_K| \cdot |W_{K'}|)$ with $K'$ the unringed nodes neither in $K$ nor joined to $K$ (quote through `<Tex>`: `\frac{|W|}{|W_K|\,|W_{K'}|}`). Figure: the face table of $B_3$ with rings $(1,0,1)$: 24, 48, 26. Provenance: verified by computation, in two ways: the counts agree with the face counts of the 26 polytopes of the table, and for `wythoff_hull_checks` (48) polytopes the exact convex hull gives the same numbers in every dimension. Attribution is open (E7): see 5.6.
4. **Two graphs on one set of points.** The move graph joins $\mu$ to $R_i\mu$ for every node $i$ (what the game does); the polytope graph joins $w\lambda$ to $wR_i\lambda$ for ringed $i$ (which vertices are neighbours in space); one multiplies by a generator on one side, the other on the other side.
