### 5.3 Rules of 4.0 that bind every tab

- Starts meaningful: the default state of every preset already shows the result (the frozen population with every city happy; the pentagon start with its level; the pair of vectors). Nothing the caption claims needs a click.
- Works before and without JavaScript: the default figure, caption and table render at build time; controls carry `disabled` until hydration; the `<noscript>` line is ToolFrame's.
- History (4.0): "Undo removes the last move from the list; redo puts it back; reset empties the list. A visible strip of past positions can be scrubbed; acting from an earlier position discards the moves after it. Because the position is recomputed, undo needs no legality test. A strip of more than 50 positions is shown as a slider with a counter." So a step, a `play on` or a hand move made while the strip shows an earlier position truncates `moves` to that position before appending, and redo is then unavailable; undo never calls `legalNodes` (after "play on" the strip can hold 20000 positions; section 5.8).
- Step and play on, quoted: "Wherever a rule fixes the next move (legal play and raising play, with the lowest-node rule), a tool offers "step", which makes one move, and "play to the end", which computes the whole play in one call (`play`, `raisingPlay`, or `playFate` off the finite types), appends its moves to the move list and shows the end. Afterwards the history holds every position of the play and can be stepped through or scrubbed, and one announcement is made for the whole play (section 7.5). Where a play need not end, the control is labelled "play on" and reports the fate (T9, T15). Neither control acts by itself on page load. Motion respects the reduced-motion setting." In the numbers tabs (`pentagon`, `three-fates`) the controls are `step` and `play on`; in `frozen` (free mutations) no rule fixes the next move, so neither control is offered.
- Keyboard and screen reader (7.5): one tab stop per board, arrows following the labels and wrapping on polygon layouts, Enter or Space acts, the polite live region with the templates of A-4.
- Signs: numeral, fill and colour (6.3), never colour alone; "may act here" by a second ring and a pointer cursor.
- Share and export: Copy link, Download data (`exportJSON` with the derived tables: level, marks, comarks, the report), Export SVG and PNG through ToolFrame (phase 2).
- Stated limits: `still running after 20000 moves` exactly (CONTRACTS 5.3); a truncated play is never presented as finished.
- Board pickers: T15 lists its own boards (section 5.1).
- Long numbers: "A value of more than 12 digits is shown shortened ("1.2345... x 10^83", with the number of digits), with the exact digits in the table view, in the tooltip and in every export." The rule applies wherever a value can pass 12 digits: a start entry typed with up to 40 digits (5.7), the level of such a start (the level never changes along a play), and node values at positive level, where the numbers grow without bound. Apply it to every shown value and test it with a typed long start (section 6), not by hoping a play grows past 12 digits.
- Small screens: usable at 320 CSS pixels; "Polygon layouts always reach 44" (A-7). The hit area is a square of side $\min(44,u)$ with $u=\min(64,\lfloor (W-16)/k\rfloor)$, $k$ the number of grid columns (6.1). A-7 names the nine-column case: "33 for nine (the affine board of $C_8$)", which is `C8~` (node 0 at $(0,0)$ left of nodes 1 to 8).
