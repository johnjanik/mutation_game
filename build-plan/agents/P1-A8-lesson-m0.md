## 9. Traps

- The roster's traps: "Write from SPEC sections 2 and 3, never from a paper; cite by key." "Node i is 'node i' or 'city i', never both in one sentence (P8)." "No em-dashes. Plain declarative sentences. Terms from Appendix C." "Credit: the mutation game is Wildberger's; retell the Mars story with credit (D5)." "The sandbox link appears automatically when phase 2 releases it; do not hand-write it."
- The bridge sentence is fixed by SPEC 2.4(a) [D]: "The number on node $i$ is the amount by which a mutation would lower city $i$." The roster's summary ("The number on a node is ...") and SPEC 3's beat text ("Each number is the amount by which a mutation would lower that city") are paraphrases; SPEC 2.4(a) wins (COMMON preamble). It is also the one sentence that names node and city together; P8 does not apply to it.
- The number is the **drop** ($a=Cp$, D7), not Wildberger's gain ($q=-Cp$). A positive number means a mutation lowers the city; a negative number means it raises. Check every sentence of beat 4 against $(0,1,0)\mapsto(0,-1,0)$ with number 2 on node 2.
- The two moves are different: a mutation changes one entry, a firing changes the entry and its neighbours (2.4(c)). Never write that the games coincide on simply laced boards, that a mutation changes the graph, or that the mutation formula acts on the weight lattice (Appendix D items 1 to 3; Appendix F).
- $-C_{ij}$ counts roads from $j$ **into** $i$ (2.1); a mutation at $i$ collects from roads into $i$, a firing at $i$ sends down roads leaving $i$. On $A_3$ both directions have one road, so a sentence with the direction wrong still gives the right numbers here; M6 will not be so forgiving, and RT-02 and RT-09 read the words. Use "into" for mutation and "leaves" for firing, as 2.2 and 2.3 do.
- `types.A3.cartan[1]` is a row; on $A_3$ it equals column 2 because $C$ is symmetric. The numbers of a population are $Cp$, a column combination. Do not generalise "row" in prose.
- $(1,1,1)$ on $A_3$ is both $\rho$ (all ones, the start of a numbers game) and the highest root (a population). They are different objects that print alike. Use `types.A3.highest_root` only for the population.
- Golden numbers in props (`caption`, `question`, `reveal`, goal text) escape the numbers-in-prose scanner. Keep golden numbers out of props, or read them through `resolveGoldenPath`.
- A digit in plain prose ("city 1", "A3") is a numbers-in-prose finding. Write labels and board names in mathematics ($A_3$, city $1$) or through `<G>`, and numbers that are words as words.
- Links: a typed `href` is not dropped by the `released` flag, and an `href` beginning with `/` breaks under `BASE_PATH=/sub/path/` (`P0-A6`'s test). Build links from the base URL, and only to released targets. In phase 1 that is M0 and M1; M1 is merged in the same stage, so `ModuleLinks` may show no next-module link in your worktree.
- `released: true` and `draft: true` are independent; the first makes the page public in the build, the second shows the mark.
- 7.6 budget: every tool in view at 360 by 800 on page open counts toward 120 kB. Keep `b1` the only tool near the top, and every other tool `client:visible`. Do not import the engine, KaTeX or anything heavy into the MDX for client use; `resolveGoldenPath` runs at build time.
- Astro 7's compiler rejects invalid HTML, and its default Markdown processor runs no remark or rehype plugins (7.2): under route (b) a bare `$...$` is plain text. Follow `decisions/P0-A3.md`.
- Predictions: the board is frozen and hints are off until the answer (4 T1). Do not set `hints` on `b2`. The answer of beat 2 is 0, which `data-value` reads as `0`.
- Undo needs no legality test and the position is recomputed from `start` and `moves` (4.0); your tests read `data-value` after every action rather than assuming the position.
- `[W-MS]` is undated; never "2003" (Appendix F). Mozes' text was not read: credit him for the numbers game, and do not describe his paper (E2). Proctor's earlier use is [S2] through Donnelly: cite [Pro84], do not quote it.
- Do not run the whole browser suite, `npm run size` or `npm run lab` (COMMON 4.3, 4.5). Run `M0.spec.ts` alone and kill `astro preview` afterwards.
- No em-dash and no en-dash anywhere you write: the page, prop strings, the test's comments, the fragment, commit messages.
