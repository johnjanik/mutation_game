# RT-13: reader-playtest

Applies from phase 1. Runs in: a worktree on branch `redteam/P{k}-RT-13`.

Before this file you have read `build-plan/RED-TEAM-COMMON.md` and `build-plan/CONTRACTS.md`. Everything there applies.

## 1. Mission

This lens plays the primary reader of SPEC 1.2: someone with first-year linear algebra ("Vectors, matrices, the idea of a reflection") and no Lie theory, who works through the released modules on the built site in reading order, does what each beat asks, commits to every prediction before its reveal, attempts every challenge, and afterwards answers the five playtest questions of SPEC 9.4 from what the site taught and from nothing else. A build passes its gate while failing this reader in ways no other test sees: the golden tests check the engine, the lesson tests check that a figure and a `<details>` exist, but nothing checks that the prose states the rule a prediction needs before it asks for it, that a prediction grades the answer the reader was taught to give, that a challenge reports success on every way of meeting its goal and on nothing else, that the result a figure demonstrates is in the text and not behind a click, or that the sentence a playtest answer depends on survives the release build. If this lens misses, the owner's playtest at G3 fails for reasons that were visible months earlier, or worse, passes while the site teaches a sign, a label or a direction wrong. The lens reports where it stopped reading, which tool it would skip, every wrong prediction and its cause, and where principles P1 to P12 cannot be pointed to.

## 4. Attack list

Each attack names the phases in which it applies. Severity follows `RED-TEAM-COMMON.md` 4 and is fixed here for this lens: a value shown to the reader that differs from the oracle, a prediction graded against a wrong answer, or a prose rule that gives the reader a wrong result, is a **blocker** (each is "a mathematical statement presented as fact that is wrong"); a playtest question that the released modules do not teach although SPEC 3 assigns the teaching to them, a result available only after a click, a broken prediction or challenge mechanism, a challenge that cannot be completed although the specification says it can ("a behaviour the specification states is missing or wrong"), a concrete consequence of 8.1 plainly absent, is **major**; wording and order slips that keep the meaning are **minor**. A principle that can be pointed to only weakly is not a finding: it goes into the principle map (attack 33) for the owner.
